==================================================
3D Changes to Ecosystems and Populations Key Terms
==================================================

The Importance of Plants in Ecosystems
======================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Erosion@@: The physical removal and transport of topsoil by natural forces such as wind or water.
    2. @@Biodiversity@@: The variety of living organisms within a given ecosystem.

----

Seasonal Patterns and Population Fluctuations
=============================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Aestivation@@: Metabolic dormancy triggered by extreme heat and arid conditions.
    2. @@Hibernation@@: Metabolic dormancy triggered by extreme cold and reduced food availability.
    3. @@Migration@@: The seasonal movement of animal populations from one region to another.

----

Ecosystem Regulation by Predators and Pollinators
=================================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Pollinator@@: An organism that transfers pollen between plants to enable fertilization and reproduction.
    2. @@Overgrazing@@: Excessive consumption of vegetation by herbivores without sufficient recovery time.
    3. @@Predator@@: An organism that hunts and feeds on other living organisms, regulating prey numbers.

----

Human Impacts: Deforestation and Agriculture
============================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Monoculture@@: The agricultural practice of growing a single crop species repeatedly over a given area.
    2. @@Eutrophication@@: Nutrient enrichment of aquatic environments leading to excessive microbial and algal growth.
    3. @@Deforestation@@: The large-scale removal or clearing of forests from a land area.
    4. @@Algal Bloom@@: The rapid proliferation of algae on the surface of a body of water.
