===========================================
3E The Impact of Invasive Species Key Terms
===========================================

Ecological Concepts and Definitions
===================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Species@@: A group of closely related organisms capable of interbreeding.
    2. @@Invasive Species@@: An introduced species that spreads rapidly and causes harm to native species or ecosystems.
    3. @@Introduced Species@@: Organisms transported beyond their natural geographical boundary by human activity.
    4. @@Eradication@@: The complete removal or destruction of a target species from an area.
    5. @@Native Species@@: Organisms that occur naturally in a specific ecological region.

----

Mechanisms of Introduction and Ecological Impact
================================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Trophic Level@@: The position an organism occupies in a food chain or food web.
    2. @@Resource Competition@@: The struggle between organisms for shared, limited resources such as food, shelter, or territory.
    3. @@Biological Control@@: The intentional release of a natural enemy or pathogen to manage a pest population.
