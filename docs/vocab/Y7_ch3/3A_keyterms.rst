=============================
3A: Nature's Pantry Key Terms
=============================

Ecosystems and Environmental Factors
====================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Abiotic Factor@@: Any non-living physical or chemical factor in an ecosystem.
    2. @@Organism@@: An individual living thing.
    3. @@Biotic Factor@@: Any living organism or biological feature within an ecosystem.
    4. @@Ecosystem@@: An environment where living organisms interact with each other and non-living elements.

----

Energy Flow and Trophic Roles
=============================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Herbivore@@: An organism that eats only plants.
    2. @@Omnivore@@: An organism that eats both plants and animals.
    3. @@Carnivore@@: An organism that eats only meat.
    4. @@Decomposer@@: An organism that breaks down dead organic material using external chemical digestion.
    5. @@Autotroph@@: An organism that makes its own food using light energy from the Sun (producer).
    6. @@Heterotroph@@: An organism that obtains energy by consuming other living or dead organisms (consumer).

----

Ecosystem Interactions
======================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Resource@@: Anything in the environment required by an organism for survival.
    2. @@Predator@@: An organism that hunts, kills, and eats other organisms for food.
    3. @@Competition@@: An interaction where organisms fight for the same limited resources.
    4. @@Prey@@: An organism that is hunted and eaten by a predator.
