=======================================
3C The Role of Microorganisms Key Terms
=======================================

Understanding Microorganisms
============================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Monera@@: A kingdom of unicellular prokaryotic organisms, such as bacteria.
    2. @@Microorganism@@: An organism that requires a microscope to be seen.
    3. @@Microscopic@@: Too small to be seen without the aid of a microscope.
    4. @@Protista@@: A diverse kingdom of eukaryotic microorganisms that do not fit into other kingdoms.

----

Microbial Producers and Consumers
=================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Phytoplankton@@: Microscopic marine photosynthetic organisms that form the base of aquatic food webs.
    2. @@Heterotroph@@: An organism that obtains energy by consuming other living or dead organisms.
    3. @@Producer@@: An organism that creates its own energy via processes like photosynthesis.

----

Microbial Decomposers and Nutrient Cycling
==========================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Nutrient Cycling@@: The movement and exchange of organic and inorganic matter back into the production of matter.
    2. @@Decomposer@@: An organism that breaks down dead organic material externally to recycle nutrients.
    3. @@Detritus@@: Waste matter in an ecosystem, including dead organisms and feces.
