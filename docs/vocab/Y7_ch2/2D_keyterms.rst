=========================================
2D Discovering the Kingdoms Key Terms
=========================================

History of Classification and Microscopy
========================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Microscope@@: An instrument used to view very small objects that cannot be seen with the naked eye.
    2. @@Microorganisms@@: Organisms that require a microscope to see, also known as microbes.

----

The Animal Kingdom
==================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Phylum@@: A rank in biological classification situated directly below kingdom.
    2. @@Invertebrates@@: Animals that lack a backbone.
    3. @@Vertebrates@@: Animals that possess a backbone.

----

The Plant Kingdom
=================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Angiosperms@@: Plants that produce flowers and seeds surrounded by a protective fruit.
    2. @@Gymnosperms@@: Non-flowering plants that produce seeds on structures such as cones.
    3. @@Spores@@: Tiny reproductive units capable of developing into a new individual independently.
    4. @@Pollination@@: The transfer of pollen between plants to enable fertilization.

----

The Protist Kingdom
===================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Protista@@: A kingdom containing organisms that do not fit into the animal, plant, or fungi kingdoms.
    2. @@Protozoa@@: Animal-like protists, such as amoebas, that can alter their shape and move to capture food.

----

The Fungi Kingdom
=================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Fungi@@: A kingdom of organisms that gain nutrition by absorbing organic matter rather than through photosynthesis.
    2. @@Yeasts@@: Single-celled microscopic fungi found on plants, in soil, and used in food production.

----

The Monera Kingdom and Three Domains
====================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Monera@@: A historical kingdom comprising microscopic organisms that lack a cell nucleus.
    2. @@Bacteria@@: Single-celled microorganisms found in widespread everyday environments.
    3. @@Archaea@@: Microscopic single-celled organisms often living in extreme conditions like acid lakes.
