2D: Discovering the Kingdoms Key Terms

How Classification Changed Over Time
====================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Binomial Nomenclature@@: A two-word naming system for organisms.
    2. @@Taxonomy@@: The scientific study of how living things are classified.
    3. @@Microscope@@: A tool used to make small objects look larger.

----

The Five Kingdoms of Life
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Heterotroph@@: An organism that consumes other living things for energy.
    2. @@Autotroph@@: An organism that makes its own food.
    3. @@Prokaryote@@: A microscopic single-celled organism without a nucleus.

----

Modern Domains and Grammar Skills
=================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Nominalisation@@: The process of changing verbs or adjectives into nouns.
    2. @@Domain@@: The highest rank of biological classification above kingdom.
