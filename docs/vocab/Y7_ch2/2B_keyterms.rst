==============================================
2B How Biologists Classify Organisms Key Terms
==============================================

The Criteria for Life
=====================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Dead@@: Organisms that once met all life processes but do not anymore.
    2. @@Respiration@@: The cellular process releasing usable energy.
    3. @@MRS GREN@@: The seven essential life processes of living things.
    4. @@Non-living@@: Objects that have never met the criteria for life.
    5. @@Sensitivity@@: The ability to detect and respond to changes.

----

Linnaean Classification and Naming
==================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Binomial Name@@: A unique two-part scientific name for an organism.
    2. @@Genus@@: A rank grouping closely related species together.
    3. @@Kingdom@@: The broadest major level in biological classification.
    4. @@Classification@@: Organizing living things into hierarchical categories.
    5. @@Species@@: The specific classification level for an individual organism.

----

Relatedness and Common Ancestors
================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Common Ancestor@@: An earlier organism from which distinct species evolved.
    2. @@Relatedness@@: How closely connected two species are by evolution.
    3. @@Traits@@: Physical or behavioral characteristics used to group organisms.
