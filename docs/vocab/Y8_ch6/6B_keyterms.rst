============================================================
6B Energy Transfers & Transformations Key Terms
============================================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@The law of conservation of energy@@: States that energy cannot be created nor destroyed.
    2. @@Energy transfer@@: Energy moves from one object to another in the same form.
    3. @@Energy transformation@@: Energy changes from one form to another inside an object.

----

Summary of Key Concepts 2
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Flow diagrams@@: Use arrows to show input energy changing into output energy.
    2. @@Hairdryer@@: Electrical energy is transformed into heat, kinetic, and sound energy.
    3. @@Mobile phone@@: Chemical energy from the battery is transformed into electrical energy.
    4. @@Sankey diagrams@@: Visually show the amount of useful energy and wasted energy in a system.
    5. @@Total input energy@@: Entering a system is always equal to the total output energy coming out.

----
