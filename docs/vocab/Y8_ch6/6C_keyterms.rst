Summary of Key Concepts 1
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Energy by-products@@: Secondary forms of energy produced during energy transfers or transformations.
    2. @@Input energy@@: The total amount of energy going into an energy transfer or transformation system.
    3. @@Output energy@@: The total energy coming out of an energy transfer or transformation system.
    4. @@Useful energy@@: Output energy that is needed and used by us for a specific purpose.
    5. @@Waste energy@@: Output energy that is not useful to us, usually released as heat, sound, or light.

----

Summary of Key Concepts 2
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Efficient@@: Means maximising useful output energy while creating the smallest possible amount of waste energy.
    2. @@Conduction@@: Transfers thermal energy through direct contact between particles in a solid, liquid, or gas.
    3. @@Convection@@: Transfers heat through the movement of liquids or gases, while radiation transfers heat via electromagnetic waves.

----
