=====================
6A Forms of Energy Key Terms
=====================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Energy@@: The ability to make changes, move objects, or do work.
    2. @@Potential energy@@: Stored energy that an object has due to its position or state.
    3. @@Kinetic energy@@: The energy of a moving object.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Thermal energy@@: The energy of moving particles inside a hot object.
    2. @@Chemical energy@@: Stored in the bonds of food, fuel, and batteries.
    3. @@Gravitational potential energy@@: Stored when an object is raised high above the ground.
    4. @@Elastic energy@@: Stored when materials like springs or rubber bands are stretched or squeezed.
    5. @@Sound energy@@: Moves through vibrating particles of air to reach our ears.
