    .. multichoice::

        What is energy defined as in physics?

        [x] The ability to make changes, move objects, or do work. | Correct! Energy is the ability to make changes, move objects, or do work.
        [ ] The measure of how fast an object moves. | Incorrect. Speed measures how fast an object moves, not its energy.
        [ ] The force required to keep an object stationary. | Incorrect. Energy relates to the capacity to do work or cause movement.
        [ ] The total mass of an object in motion. | Incorrect. Mass is the amount of matter in an object, not energy.

    .. multichoice::

        What type of energy does a moving object possess?

        [ ] Potential energy | Incorrect. Potential energy is stored energy based on position or state.
        [x] Kinetic energy | Correct! Kinetic energy is the energy of a moving object.
        [ ] Elastic energy | Incorrect. Elastic energy is stored in stretched or compressed materials.
        [ ] Chemical energy | Incorrect. Chemical energy is stored in the chemical bonds of substances.

    .. multichoice::

        Where is chemical energy stored?

        [ ] In vibrating air particles | Incorrect. Sound energy travels through vibrating air particles.
        [ ] In objects raised high above the ground | Incorrect. Raising an object increases its gravitational potential energy.
        [x] In the bonds of food, fuel, and batteries. | Correct! Chemical energy is stored within chemical bonds.
        [ ] Inside moving charged particles powering devices | Incorrect. Moving charged particles carry electrical energy.

    .. multichoice::

        What form of energy is stored when a rubber band or spring is stretched or compressed?

        [ ] Gravitational potential energy | Incorrect. Gravitational energy depends on height above the ground.
        [x] Elastic energy | Correct! Elastic energy is stored when materials are stretched or squeezed.
        [ ] Thermal energy | Incorrect. Thermal energy is the energy of moving particles inside a hot object.
        [ ] Radiant energy | Incorrect. Radiant energy travels in waves like light or heat.

    .. multichoice::

        When an object is raised high above the ground, what form of energy does it gain?

        [x] Gravitational potential energy | Correct! Gravitational potential energy is stored when an object is elevated.
        [ ] Elastic energy | Incorrect. Elastic energy requires stretching or compressing materials.
        [ ] Sound energy | Incorrect. Sound energy requires vibrating particles.
        [ ] Nuclear energy | Incorrect. Nuclear energy comes from combining or splitting atomic nuclei.

    .. multichoice::

        Thermal energy is best described as:

        [ ] Energy stored in atomic nuclei | Incorrect. Nuclear energy is stored in atomic nuclei.
        [x] The energy of moving particles inside a hot object. | Correct! Thermal energy (heat) is the movement of particles within hot substances.
        [ ] Energy that travels through space without particles | Incorrect. Light energy can travel through space without a medium.
        [ ] Energy stored due to an object's position | Incorrect. Position-based stored energy is potential energy.

    .. multichoice::

        How does sound energy travel to reach our ears?

        [ ] Through empty space without any particles | Incorrect. Sound requires a medium and cannot travel through a vacuum.
        [ ] By splitting atomic bonds | Incorrect. Splitting atomic bonds releases nuclear energy.
        [x] Through vibrating particles of air. | Correct! Sound energy moves by causing air particles to vibrate.
        [ ] By storing energy in stretched rubber bands | Incorrect. Stretched materials store elastic energy.

    .. multichoice::

        Which form of energy is carried by moving charged particles to power electronic devices?

        [x] Electrical energy | Correct! Electrical energy comes from the movement of charged particles.
        [ ] Mechanical energy | Incorrect. Mechanical energy is the energy of moving physical parts in machines.
        [ ] Chemical energy | Incorrect. Chemical energy is stored in chemical bonds before being released.
        [ ] Radiant energy | Incorrect. Radiant energy travels in waves such as light.

    .. multichoice::

        What type of energy is released when atoms are combined or split apart?

        [ ] Chemical energy | Incorrect. Chemical energy involves molecular bonds, not splitting or fusing atomic nuclei.
        [ ] Radiant energy | Incorrect. Radiant energy is energy traveling in electromagnetic waves.
        [x] Nuclear energy | Correct! Nuclear energy is released by fusing or splitting atomic nuclei.
        [ ] Thermal energy | Incorrect. While thermal energy produces heat, nuclear energy is the specific source from atomic reaction.

    .. multichoice::

        Which form of energy can travel at high speed through space without requiring particles?

        [ ] Sound energy | Incorrect. Sound energy requires a physical medium like air to travel.
        [x] Light energy | Correct! Light energy can travel through the vacuum of space without particles.
        [ ] Thermal energy | Incorrect. Thermal energy within an object is defined by particle motion.
        [ ] Mechanical energy | Incorrect. Mechanical energy involves the movement of physical parts.
