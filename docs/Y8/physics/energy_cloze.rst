====================================================
Energy Cloze
====================================================

.. cloze::

    #. @@Energy@@ is the ability to move or make changes to the shape of an object, or to do work.
    #. @@Work@@ is the amount of energy that an object transfers to another object.
    #. Energy is categorized into two main types: @@potential energy@@ and @@kinetic energy@@.
    #. @@Potential energy@@ is stored energy that an object has due to its position or state.
    #. @@Kinetic energy@@ is the movement energy that an object has when it is actively in motion.


.. cloze::

    #. @@Gravitational potential energy@@ (GPE) is stored energy an object gains when raised above the ground, giving it the capacity to fall.
    #. @@Conservation of energy@@ refers to the concept that the total amount of energy in a system remains constant over time.
    #. When an object falls, its @@gravitational potential energy@@ transforms directly into @@kinetic energy@@.
    #. In dynamic systems like a roller coaster going downhill, total energy is conserved as @@potential energy@@ decreases while @@kinetic energy@@ increases.


.. cloze::

    #. @@Chemical energy@@ is stored energy in the chemical bonds of compounds and molecules, including food, fuels, and batteries.
    #. @@Chemical energy@@ is released as through chemical reactions.
    #. @@Electrical energy@@ is carried by the movement of charged particles, making it a form of @@kinetic energy@@.
    #. @@Static electricity@@ occurs when charged particles build up on a surface until they are suddenly released.
    #. @@Elastic energy@@ is stored energy in materials that are stretched or compressed, such as rubber bands, springs, and bouncing balls.
    #. @@Nuclear energy@@ is the energy stored inside atoms and is released by breaking atoms apart or combining them together.


.. cloze::

    #. @@Thermal energy@@ is a form of kinetic energy caused by the motion of particles within a substance.
    #. @@Heat energy@@ refers specifically to @@thermal energy@@ being transferred from a hotter object to a cooler object.
    #. @@Sound energy@@ is a kinetic form of energy produced by vibrating objects that travel through air particles as sound waves.
    #. Forms of @@electro magnetic radiation@@ (EMR) include @@visible light@@, @@ultraviolet@@ light, @@radio waves@@, and @@infrared@@ light.
