====================================================
Energy Quick Questions
====================================================

.. cloze::

    #. What is the ability to make changes to our world called? — @@Energy@@
    #. What are the two main categories of energy? — @@Potential and kinetic@@
    #. What type of energy is stored due to an object's position or state? — @@Potential energy@@
    #. What is stored energy called when an object is raised above the ground? — @@Gravitational potential energy@@
    #. What force pulls objects towards Earth? — @@Gravity@@
    #. Which scientist was inspired by a falling apple to study gravity? — @@Isaac Newton@@


.. cloze::

    #. When a raised object falls, what does its gravitational potential energy transform into? — @@Kinetic energy@@
    #. On a roller coaster, where is potential energy at its highest? — @@Highest point@@
    #. On a roller coaster, where is kinetic energy at its highest? — @@Lowest point@@
    #. What happens to potential energy as kinetic energy increases in a system? — @@It decreases@@


.. cloze::

    #. What type of energy does an object have due to being in motion? — @@Kinetic energy@@
    #. What form of kinetic energy do moving particles within a substance have? — @@Thermal energy@@
    #. What is thermal energy called when it is being transferred between objects? — @@Heat energy@@
    #. What is a measure of the average kinetic energy of particles in a substance? — @@Temperature@@
    #. Which celestial object combines atoms to release heat and light? — @@The Sun@@
    #. In which direction does heat energy always transfer? — @@Hot to cold@@


.. cloze::

    #. What type of energy is stored in materials that stretch, bounce, or spring back? — @@Elastic energy@@
    #. What word describes squeezing or pressing a material together? — @@Compressing@@
    #. What type of energy is released when atoms are combined or split apart? — @@Nuclear energy@@
    #. What type of energy is found in the bonds of compounds and molecules? — @@Chemical energy@@
    #. What is one everyday source of chemical energy for animals? — @@Food@@
    #. What is the energy carried by charged particles called? — @@Electrical energy@@
    #. What is charged particles built up on a surface until released called? — @@Static electricity@@
    #. What kind of energy do a battery and a banana both have in common? — @@Chemical potential energy@@


.. cloze::

    #. What happens to electrical energy in a video game console? — @@Changes to sound and light@@
    #. What is the energy carried by sound waves called? — @@Sound energy@@
    #. What word describes the back-and-forth motion of particles? — @@Vibrations@@
    #. What is the energy carried by tiny particles called photons? — @@Light energy@@
    #. Is light energy classified as potential or kinetic energy? — @@Neither@@
    #. What type of radiation can humans sense as heat but not see? — @@Infrared@@
