====================================================
Electricity Embedded Clauses
====================================================

Embedded Clauses as Qualifiers in Noun Groups
--------------------------------------------------------------

| Qualifers are words or phrases that provide additional information about a noun. In this section, we will focus on embedded clauses that serve as Qualifiers in noun groups. An embedded clause is a dependent clause that is inserted within a noun group to give more detail about the Thing being described.
| Embedded clauses can be introduced by relative pronouns such as "that," "which," or "who," and they provide essential or non-essential information about the noun.

Noun Group = :premodifier:`any premodifiers` + :thing:`Thing` + :embedded:`Qualifier`
