    .. multichoice::

        What does MRS GREN stand for?

        [x] The seven essential life processes of living things. | Correct! MRS GREN lists the seven criteria for life.
        [ ] The seven levels of classification. | Incorrect. Classification levels run from kingdom to species.
        [ ] The two parts of a binomial name. | Incorrect. A binomial name is the genus and species.
        [ ] The three domains of life. | Incorrect. MRS GREN describes life processes, not domains.

    .. multichoice::

        What is respiration?

        [ ] The ability to detect and respond to changes. | Incorrect. That describes sensitivity.
        [ ] Removing waste from the body. | Incorrect. That describes excretion.
        [x] The cellular process that releases usable energy. | Correct! Respiration breaks down food to release energy.
        [ ] Increasing in body size. | Incorrect. That describes growth.

    .. multichoice::

        What is sensitivity?

        [ ] Creating offspring. | Incorrect. That describes reproduction.
        [x] The ability to detect and respond to changes. | Correct! Sensitivity lets organisms react to their environment.
        [ ] Getting food materials from the environment. | Incorrect. That describes nutrients.
        [ ] Moving body parts. | Incorrect. That describes movement.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the difference between a non-living object and a dead organism?

        [ ] There is no difference. | Incorrect. A dead organism was once alive; a non-living object never was.
        [ ] Non-living objects met the criteria for life in the past. | Incorrect. Non-living objects never met all seven criteria.
        [ ] Dead organisms still meet all seven life processes. | Incorrect. Dead organisms no longer carry out life processes.
        [x] A dead organism met all life processes in the past; a non-living object never did. | Correct! Only dead organisms were once alive.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the broadest level in biological classification?

        [x] Kingdom | Correct! Kingdom is the broadest major level.
        [ ] Species | Incorrect. Species is the most specific level.
        [ ] Genus | Incorrect. Genus groups closely related species.
        [ ] Binomial | Incorrect. A binomial name is a two-part scientific name, not a level.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a binomial name?

        [ ] The name of a kingdom. | Incorrect. A binomial name identifies a single species.
        [ ] A common nickname for an animal. | Incorrect. Binomial names are scientific names.
        [x] A unique two-part scientific name using the genus and species. | Correct! Every organism has a two-part binomial name.
        [ ] A list of seven classification levels. | Incorrect. That describes the classification hierarchy.

    .. multichoice::

        How is a binomial name correctly written?

        [ ] Both parts capitalized and in bold. | Incorrect. Only the genus is capitalized, and the name is in italics.
        [x] In italics, with the genus capitalized and the species in lowercase. | Correct! For example, *Homo sapiens*.
        [ ] Species first, then genus, all in capitals. | Incorrect. The genus comes first.
        [ ] In lowercase with no italics. | Incorrect. The genus is capitalized and the name is italicized.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a genus?

        [ ] The broadest level of classification. | Incorrect. That is the kingdom.
        [ ] A single individual organism. | Incorrect. A genus contains several related species.
        [ ] The ability to respond to changes. | Incorrect. That describes sensitivity.
        [x] A rank grouping closely related species together. | Correct! A genus groups similar species.

    .. multichoice::

        What does it mean if two organisms share more classification levels?

        [x] They are more closely related and share a recent common ancestor. | Correct! More shared levels means closer relatedness.
        [ ] They are not related at all. | Incorrect. Shared levels show relatedness.
        [ ] They must be the same species. | Incorrect. They can share many levels but still be different species.
        [ ] One must be living and the other dead. | Incorrect. Classification levels do not show whether something is alive.

    .. multichoice::

        Which animal do humans share a closer common ancestor with?

        [ ] Kangaroos | Incorrect. Humans are more distantly related to kangaroos.
        [ ] Fish | Incorrect. Fish are more distantly related to humans than other primates are.
        [x] Bonobos | Correct! Humans share a more recent common ancestor with bonobos.
        [ ] Birds | Incorrect. Birds are more distantly related to humans.
