===========================================
2A Introduction to Classification Key Terms
===========================================

What is Classification?
=======================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Taxonomy@@: The science of classifying and naming organisms.
    2. @@Taxonomist@@: A scientist who organizes living things into categories.
    3. @@Organism@@: An individual living thing.
    4. @@Classification@@: The process of grouping things together.
    5. @@Species@@: A group of closely related organisms.

----

Steps and Importance of Classification
======================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Conservation@@: Protecting organisms and natural resources for the future.
    2. @@Endangered@@: At risk of dying out or becoming extinct.
    3. @@Extinction@@: The complete dying out of a species.
    4. @@Similarities@@: Characteristics or traits that are the same.
