        What is classification?

        [x] The process of sorting organisms into groups based on shared features and differences. | Correct! Classification groups living things by their similarities and differences.
        [ ] The complete dying out of a species. | Incorrect. That describes extinction.
        [ ] Protecting organisms and natural resources for the future. | Incorrect. That describes conservation.
        [ ] A scientist who organizes living things into categories. | Incorrect. That describes a taxonomist.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the science of studying, grouping, and naming organisms called?

        [ ] Conservation | Incorrect. Conservation is about protecting organisms and resources.
        [x] Taxonomy | Correct! Taxonomy is the science of classifying and naming organisms.
        [ ] Extinction | Incorrect. Extinction is the complete dying out of a species.
        [ ] Endangerment | Incorrect. Endangered describes a species at risk of dying out.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a taxonomist?

        [ ] An individual living thing. | Incorrect. That describes an organism.
        [ ] A group of closely related organisms. | Incorrect. That describes a species.
        [x] A scientist who organizes living things into categories. | Correct! Taxonomists study and group organisms.
        [ ] A species at risk of dying out. | Incorrect. That describes an endangered species.

    .. multichoice::

        What is an organism?

        [ ] A group of closely related species. | Incorrect. An organism is a single living thing.
        [ ] A non-living part of the environment. | Incorrect. Organisms are living things.
        [ ] The process of naming living things. | Incorrect. Naming living things is part of taxonomy.
        [x] An individual living thing. | Correct! An organism is any individual living thing.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a species?

        [x] A group of closely related organisms. | Correct! A species is a group of closely related living things.
        [ ] A scientist who classifies organisms. | Incorrect. That describes a taxonomist.
        [ ] The process of grouping things together. | Incorrect. That describes classification.
        [ ] Protecting organisms for the future. | Incorrect. That describes conservation.

    .. multichoice::

        When scientists discover a new species, what do they do?

        [ ] Immediately declare it endangered. | Incorrect. Being new does not make a species endangered.
        [x] Look at its unique traits to see if it fits an existing group or needs a new one. | Correct! Its traits decide which group it belongs to.
        [ ] Remove it from its habitat. | Incorrect. Classification does not require removing organisms.
        [ ] Give it the same name as a similar species. | Incorrect. Each species has its own name.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the first step scientists take to classify an organism?

        [ ] Choose the best group straight away. | Incorrect. Choosing the group comes last.
        [ ] Decide whether it is endangered. | Incorrect. This is not a step in classifying an organism.
        [x] Find its similarities and differences. | Correct! Scientists first compare similarities and differences.
        [ ] Count how many of the organism exist. | Incorrect. Population size is not the first classification step.

    .. multichoice::

        Which of these is a reason classification is important?

        [ ] It makes organisms grow faster. | Incorrect. Classification does not affect growth.
        [ ] It stops new species from being discovered. | Incorrect. Classification helps organize newly discovered species.
        [ ] It changes an organism's features. | Incorrect. Classification describes features; it does not change them.
        [x] It helps scientists understand survival needs and how closely organisms are related. | Correct! Grouping species reveals their needs and relationships.

    .. multichoice::

        What does endangered mean?

        [x] At risk of dying out or becoming extinct. | Correct! Endangered species are at risk of extinction.
        [ ] Already completely died out. | Incorrect. That describes an extinct species.
        [ ] Protected for the future. | Incorrect. That describes conservation.
        [ ] Closely related to another species. | Incorrect. Relatedness is not the same as being endangered.

    .. multichoice::

        What is conservation?

        [ ] The complete dying out of a species. | Incorrect. That describes extinction.
        [ ] Sorting organisms into groups. | Incorrect. That describes classification.
        [x] Protecting organisms and natural resources for the future. | Correct! Conservation aims to protect species and nature.
        [ ] Naming newly discovered species. | Incorrect. Naming organisms is part of taxonomy.
