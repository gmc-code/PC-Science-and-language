    .. multichoice::

        What invention in the 1600s allowed scientists to observe microscopic life?

        [x] The microscope | Correct! Microscopes revealed cells and microorganisms.
        [ ] The telescope | Incorrect. Telescopes are used to view distant objects in space.
        [ ] DNA analysis | Incorrect. DNA analysis is a modern technique.
        [ ] The thermometer | Incorrect. Thermometers measure temperature.

    .. multichoice::

        What are microorganisms?

        [ ] Animals that lack a backbone. | Incorrect. Those are invertebrates.
        [x] Organisms that require a microscope to see, also known as microbes. | Correct! Microorganisms are too small to see with the naked eye.
        [ ] Plants that produce flowers. | Incorrect. Those are angiosperms.
        [ ] Organisms that live only in hot springs. | Incorrect. Microorganisms live in many different environments.

    .. multichoice::

        Which phylum do all vertebrates belong to?

        [ ] Protista | Incorrect. Protista is a kingdom of microorganisms.
        [ ] Monera | Incorrect. Monera was a kingdom for organisms without a nucleus.
        [x] Chordata | Correct! All vertebrates belong to the phylum Chordata.
        [ ] Fungi | Incorrect. Fungi is a separate kingdom.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the difference between invertebrates and vertebrates?

        [ ] Invertebrates make their own food; vertebrates do not. | Incorrect. Both are animals and consume other organisms.
        [ ] Vertebrates are microscopic; invertebrates are not. | Incorrect. Size is not what separates these groups.
        [ ] Invertebrates are plants; vertebrates are animals. | Incorrect. Both groups are animals.
        [x] Invertebrates lack a backbone; vertebrates have one. | Correct! The backbone separates the two groups.

    .. multichoice::

        What are angiosperms?

        [x] Plants that produce flowers and seeds surrounded by protective fruit. | Correct! Angiosperms are flowering plants.
        [ ] Plants that produce seeds on cones. | Incorrect. Those are gymnosperms.
        [ ] Plants that reproduce using spores. | Incorrect. Ferns and mosses reproduce using spores.
        [ ] Single-celled fungi. | Incorrect. Those are yeasts.

    .. multichoice::

        How do non-flowering plants like ferns and mosses reproduce?

        [ ] Using seeds inside fruit. | Incorrect. Angiosperms produce seeds inside fruit.
        [ ] Using seeds on cones. | Incorrect. Gymnosperms produce seeds on cones.
        [x] Using tiny units called spores. | Correct! Ferns and mosses reproduce using spores.
        [ ] By absorbing decaying matter. | Incorrect. That is how fungi get nutrition.

    .. multichoice::

        Why was the kingdom Protista created?

        [ ] To group all flowering plants. | Incorrect. Flowering plants belong to the plant kingdom.
        [x] To house organisms with a mix of plant and animal features that did not fit elsewhere. | Correct! Protists like *Euglena* blur the line between plants and animals.
        [ ] To group animals with backbones. | Incorrect. Those are vertebrates in the animal kingdom.
        [ ] To group bacteria without a nucleus. | Incorrect. That was the kingdom Monera.

    .. multichoice::

        Why were fungi removed from the plant kingdom?

        [ ] Because they grow out of the ground. | Incorrect. Growing from the ground is why they were first thought to be plants.
        [ ] Because they have backbones. | Incorrect. Fungi do not have backbones.
        [ ] Because they reproduce using seeds. | Incorrect. Fungi do not produce seeds.
        [x] Because they absorb nutrients from decaying matter instead of using sunlight. | Correct! Fungi do not photosynthesize like plants.

    .. multichoice::

        What are archaea?

        [ ] Multicellular fungi such as mushrooms. | Incorrect. Mushrooms belong to the fungi kingdom.
        [ ] Animal-like protists such as amoebas. | Incorrect. Those are protozoa.
        [x] Single-celled organisms often living in extreme environments like hot springs. | Correct! Archaea thrive in extreme conditions.
        [ ] Plants that produce seeds on cones. | Incorrect. Those are gymnosperms.

    .. multichoice::

        What are the three domains used in modern classification?

        [x] Bacteria, Archaea, and Eukarya | Correct! DNA evidence organizes life into these three domains.
        [ ] Plants, Animals, and Fungi | Incorrect. These are kingdoms within the domain Eukarya.
        [ ] Monera, Protista, and Fungi | Incorrect. These are kingdoms, not domains.
        [ ] Vertebrates, Invertebrates, and Protozoa | Incorrect. These are groups of organisms, not domains.
