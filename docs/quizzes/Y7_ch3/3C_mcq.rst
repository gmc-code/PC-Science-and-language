    .. multichoice::

        What is a microorganism?

        [x] An organism that requires a microscope to be seen. | Correct! Microorganisms are also called microbes.
        [ ] Waste matter including dead organisms and faeces. | Incorrect. That describes detritus.
        [ ] An animal at the top of a food chain. | Incorrect. That describes an apex predator.
        [ ] A large plant that produces seeds. | Incorrect. Microorganisms are too small to see without a microscope.

    .. multichoice::

        Which three kingdoms do microorganisms come from?

        [ ] Plants, Animals, and Fungi | Incorrect. Plants and animals are not mainly microscopic.
        [x] Monera, Fungi, and Protista | Correct! Microbes originate from these three kingdoms.
        [ ] Vertebrates, Invertebrates, and Fungi | Incorrect. These are not all kingdoms.
        [ ] Bacteria, Plants, and Animals | Incorrect. Plants and animals are not mainly microscopic.

    .. multichoice::

        Which kingdom do bacteria such as *Salmonella* belong to?

        [ ] Fungi | Incorrect. Fungi include mushrooms, moulds, and yeasts.
        [ ] Protista | Incorrect. Protista includes algae and protozoa.
        [x] Monera | Correct! Monera contains prokaryotic organisms such as bacteria.
        [ ] Plantae | Incorrect. Bacteria are not plants.

    .. multichoice::

        What three ecological roles can microorganisms fulfil?

        [ ] Predators, prey, and parasites | Incorrect. The three roles are based on how microbes get energy.
        [ ] Herbivores, carnivores, and omnivores | Incorrect. These describe the diets of consumers.
        [ ] Producers, keystone species, and apex predators | Incorrect. Microbes do not act as apex predators.
        [x] Producers, consumers, and decomposers | Correct! Microbes fill all three roles.

    .. multichoice::

        What are phytoplankton?

        [x] Microscopic photosynthetic organisms that form the base of aquatic food webs. | Correct! Phytoplankton are primary producers.
        [ ] Bacteria that cause disease. | Incorrect. Phytoplankton make food using sunlight.
        [ ] Fungi that break down dead matter. | Incorrect. That describes decomposers.
        [ ] Large marine predators. | Incorrect. Phytoplankton are microscopic producers.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a microbial heterotroph?

        [ ] A microbe that makes its own food from sunlight. | Incorrect. That is a producer.
        [x] A microbe that obtains energy by consuming other organisms. | Correct! Heterotrophs are consumers.
        [ ] A microbe that only lives in soil. | Incorrect. Heterotrophs are defined by how they get energy.
        [ ] A microbe that never needs energy. | Incorrect. All organisms need energy.

    .. multichoice::

        What is detritus?

        [ ] Microscopic marine producers. | Incorrect. Those are phytoplankton.
        [ ] Minerals released by plants. | Incorrect. Detritus is waste and dead matter.
        [x] Waste matter, including dead organisms and faeces. | Correct! Decomposers break down detritus.
        [ ] A kingdom of microorganisms. | Incorrect. Detritus is waste, not a kingdom.

    .. multichoice::

        How do microbial decomposers such as fungi and bacteria break down detritus?

        [ ] By photosynthesis. | Incorrect. Photosynthesis is how producers make food.
        [ ] By hunting it like a predator. | Incorrect. Decomposers do not hunt.
        [ ] By swallowing it whole. | Incorrect. Decomposers digest matter outside their bodies.
        [x] By breaking it down externally and absorbing the nutrients. | Correct! Decomposers use external digestion.

    .. multichoice::

        From which trophic levels does energy flow to decomposers?

        [x] All trophic levels | Correct! Every organism eventually dies or produces waste.
        [ ] Only producers | Incorrect. Decomposers break down matter from every level.
        [ ] Only apex predators | Incorrect. Decomposers break down matter from every level.
        [ ] No trophic levels | Incorrect. Decomposers receive energy from dead matter and waste.

    .. multichoice::

        What would happen to an ecosystem without decomposers?

        [ ] Plants would grow faster. | Incorrect. Without recycled nutrients, plant growth would stop.
        [ ] Nothing would change. | Incorrect. Decomposers are essential to ecosystems.
        [x] Waste would build up, blocking sunlight and air, stopping plant growth and causing collapse. | Correct! Decomposers recycle nutrients that producers need.
        [ ] There would be more nutrients in the soil. | Incorrect. Decomposers are what return nutrients to the soil.
