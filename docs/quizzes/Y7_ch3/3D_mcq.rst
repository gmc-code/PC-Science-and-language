    .. multichoice::

        What gas do plants release that helps maintain ecosystem balance?

        [x] Oxygen | Correct! Plants absorb carbon dioxide and release oxygen.
        [ ] Carbon dioxide | Incorrect. Plants take in carbon dioxide.
        [ ] Nitrogen | Incorrect. Plants do not release nitrogen as part of this process.
        [ ] Methane | Incorrect. Plants do not release methane as part of this process.

    .. multichoice::

        How do tree roots help terrestrial ecosystems?

        [ ] They cause soil erosion. | Incorrect. Roots help prevent erosion.
        [x] They stabilize soil and reduce erosion. | Correct! Root networks hold topsoil in place.
        [ ] They block water from entering the soil. | Incorrect. That is caused by soil compaction.
        [ ] They remove nutrients from the soil permanently. | Incorrect. Trees replenish nutrients when they decompose.

    .. multichoice::

        What is biodiversity?

        [ ] The removal of topsoil by wind or water. | Incorrect. That describes erosion.
        [ ] Growing a single crop repeatedly. | Incorrect. That describes monoculture.
        [x] The variety of living organisms within an ecosystem. | Correct! Biodiverse ecosystems contain many different species.
        [ ] The seasonal movement of animals. | Incorrect. That describes migration.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the difference between hibernation and aestivation?

        [ ] Hibernation is triggered by heat; aestivation by cold. | Incorrect. It is the other way around.
        [ ] Both describe animals moving to new regions. | Incorrect. That describes migration.
        [ ] They are both types of farming. | Incorrect. They are types of dormancy.
        [x] Hibernation is triggered by extreme cold; aestivation by extreme heat and dry conditions. | Correct! Both are dormancy, triggered by opposite conditions.

    .. multichoice::

        Humpback whales travel to warmer northern waters to raise their young. What is this an example of?

        [x] Migration | Correct! Migration is the seasonal movement of animals.
        [ ] Hibernation | Incorrect. Hibernation is dormancy in cold conditions.
        [ ] Overgrazing | Incorrect. Overgrazing is excessive eating of vegetation.
        [ ] Eutrophication | Incorrect. Eutrophication is nutrient overloading in water.

    .. multichoice::

        What happens to an ecosystem when pollinators disappear?

        [ ] Plants reproduce faster. | Incorrect. Pollinators are needed for plant reproduction.
        [ ] Predator numbers increase. | Incorrect. Food shortages reduce consumers at every level.
        [x] Plant reproduction stops, causing food shortages that ripple up through consumers. | Correct! Pollinators support the base of the food web.
        [ ] Soil erosion stops. | Incorrect. Losing pollinators does not stop erosion.

    .. multichoice::

        What happens when top predators are removed from an ecosystem?

        [ ] Prey populations decrease. | Incorrect. Without predators, prey numbers grow.
        [x] Prey populations increase rapidly, leading to overgrazing. | Correct! Too many herbivores deplete vegetation.
        [ ] Vegetation grows much faster. | Incorrect. Vegetation is depleted by overgrazing.
        [ ] Pollinators disappear. | Incorrect. Removing predators mainly affects prey numbers.

    .. multichoice::

        What is monoculture?

        [ ] The clearing of forests from an area. | Incorrect. That describes deforestation.
        [ ] Rapid growth of algae on water. | Incorrect. That describes an algal bloom.
        [ ] Animals moving between regions. | Incorrect. That describes migration.
        [x] Growing a single crop species repeatedly over an area. | Correct! Monoculture depletes soil nutrients.

    .. multichoice::

        How do heavy livestock affect soil?

        [x] They compact the soil, limiting root growth and water absorption. | Correct! Compaction increases runoff and erosion.
        [ ] They add oxygen to the soil. | Incorrect. Compaction reduces air spaces in soil.
        [ ] They prevent soil erosion. | Incorrect. Compaction increases runoff and erosion.
        [ ] They have no effect on soil. | Incorrect. Heavy livestock squash soil particles together.

    .. multichoice::

        What is eutrophication?

        [ ] Dormancy caused by extreme heat. | Incorrect. That describes aestivation.
        [ ] The removal of forests for farming. | Incorrect. That describes deforestation.
        [x] Nutrient enrichment of water causing algal blooms that block sunlight and deplete oxygen. | Correct! Fertilizer runoff causes eutrophication.
        [ ] The transfer of pollen between plants. | Incorrect. That describes pollination.
