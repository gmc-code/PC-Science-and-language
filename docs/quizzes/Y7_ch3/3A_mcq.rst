    .. multichoice::

        What is an ecosystem?

        [x] An environment where living organisms interact with each other and non-living elements. | Correct! Ecosystems include both living and non-living parts.
        [ ] A single living thing. | Incorrect. That describes an organism.
        [ ] An organism that eats only plants. | Incorrect. That describes a herbivore.
        [ ] A non-living factor such as sunlight. | Incorrect. That describes an abiotic factor.

    .. multichoice::

        Which of these is a biotic factor?

        [ ] Sunlight | Incorrect. Sunlight is a non-living (abiotic) factor.
        [ ] Temperature | Incorrect. Temperature is a non-living (abiotic) factor.
        [x] Bacteria | Correct! Bacteria are living, so they are a biotic factor.
        [ ] Water | Incorrect. Water is a non-living (abiotic) factor.

    .. multichoice::

        What is an abiotic factor?

        [ ] Any living organism in an ecosystem. | Incorrect. That describes a biotic factor.
        [x] Any non-living physical or chemical factor in an ecosystem. | Correct! Examples include sunlight, temperature, and water.
        [ ] An animal that hunts other animals. | Incorrect. That describes a predator.
        [ ] An organism that breaks down dead matter. | Incorrect. That describes a decomposer.

    .. multichoice::

        What is an autotroph?

        [ ] An organism that eats only meat. | Incorrect. That describes a carnivore.
        [ ] An organism that consumes other organisms for energy. | Incorrect. That describes a heterotroph.
        [ ] An organism that is hunted by a predator. | Incorrect. That describes prey.
        [x] An organism that makes its own food using light energy from the Sun. | Correct! Autotrophs are producers.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a heterotroph?

        [x] An organism that obtains energy by consuming other living or dead organisms. | Correct! Heterotrophs are consumers.
        [ ] An organism that makes its own food from sunlight. | Incorrect. That describes an autotroph.
        [ ] A non-living part of an ecosystem. | Incorrect. That describes an abiotic factor.
        [ ] A resource needed for survival. | Incorrect. Heterotrophs are organisms, not resources.

    .. multichoice::

        What is an omnivore?

        [ ] An organism that eats only plants. | Incorrect. That describes a herbivore.
        [ ] An organism that eats only meat. | Incorrect. That describes a carnivore.
        [x] An organism that eats both plants and animals. | Correct! Omnivores have a mixed diet.
        [ ] An organism that makes its own food. | Incorrect. That describes an autotroph.

    .. multichoice::

        What role do decomposers like fungi play in an ecosystem?

        [ ] They hunt and eat other animals. | Incorrect. That is the role of predators.
        [x] They break down dead organic material using external digestion. | Correct! Decomposers are part of Earth's cleanup crew.
        [ ] They make food from sunlight. | Incorrect. That is the role of producers.
        [ ] They compete for mates. | Incorrect. That is not what decomposers do in an ecosystem.

    .. multichoice::

        When does competition between organisms occur?

        [ ] When resources are unlimited. | Incorrect. Plentiful resources reduce competition.
        [ ] Only between predators and prey. | Incorrect. Predator and prey interact by hunting, not competing.
        [ ] Only during winter. | Incorrect. Competition can happen at any time of year.
        [x] When vital resources such as food, water, light, space, or mates are limited. | Correct! Limited resources cause competition.

    .. multichoice::

        How does competition affect the organisms involved?

        [x] It negatively affects both because both must use extra energy. | Correct! Competition costs energy for all competitors.
        [ ] It only benefits the winner with no cost. | Incorrect. Both competitors use extra energy.
        [ ] It has no effect on either organism. | Incorrect. Competition affects survival.
        [ ] It increases the resources available. | Incorrect. Competition happens because resources are limited.

    .. multichoice::

        How do predator-prey interactions affect an ecosystem?

        [ ] They cause all prey to die out. | Incorrect. Predators and prey usually stay in balance.
        [ ] They have no effect on population sizes. | Incorrect. They directly affect population sizes.
        [x] They keep population sizes balanced over time. | Correct! Predators and prey regulate each other's numbers.
        [ ] They change abiotic factors like temperature. | Incorrect. Predator-prey interactions do not change the weather.
