======================================
3B Food Chains and Food Webs Key Terms
======================================

Understanding Food Chains
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Apex Predator@@: An animal at the top of a food chain with no natural predators.
    2. @@Food Chain@@: A single sequence showing how energy passes from one organism to another through feeding.
    3. @@Trophic Level@@: The specific step or position an organism occupies in a food sequence.
    4. @@Producer@@: An autotroph that converts sunlight into chemical energy/food.

----

Energy Transfer and Trophic Pyramids
====================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Primary Consumer@@: An organism that feeds directly on producers (herbivore).
    2. @@Secondary Consumer@@: An organism that feeds on primary consumers.
    3. @@Trophic Pyramid@@: A diagram showing the decrease in available energy at each progressive trophic level.

----

Food Webs and Ecosystem Dynamics
================================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Trophic Cascade@@: Side-effect events that ripple through all trophic levels when a top organism is added or removed.
    2. @@Food Web@@: A complex network of interconnected food chains showing multiple energy pathways.
    3. @@Biodiverse@@: Containing a wide variety of different living organisms within an ecosystem.
    4. @@Keystone Species@@: A species that has an unusually large effect on the balance and health of its ecosystem.
