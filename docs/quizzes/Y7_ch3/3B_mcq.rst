3B Food Chains and Food Webs MCQ

    .. multichoice::

        What is a food chain?

        [x] A single sequence showing how energy passes from one organism to another through feeding. | Correct! A food chain shows one pathway of energy flow.
        [ ] A complex network of interconnected feeding pathways. | Incorrect. That describes a food web.
        [ ] A diagram showing energy decreasing at each level. | Incorrect. That describes a trophic pyramid.
        [ ] A species with a large effect on its ecosystem. | Incorrect. That describes a keystone species.

    .. multichoice::

        What does every food chain begin with?

        [ ] An apex predator | Incorrect. Apex predators are at the top of the food chain.
        [x] A producer | Correct! Producers capture light energy from the Sun to make sugar.
        [ ] A secondary consumer | Incorrect. Secondary consumers eat primary consumers.
        [ ] A decomposer | Incorrect. Food chains begin with the organism that captures the Sun's energy.

    .. multichoice::

        What do the arrows in a food chain show?

        [ ] Which animal is the largest. | Incorrect. Arrows have nothing to do with size.
        [ ] Which organism is hunted. | Incorrect. Arrows point to the eater, not the eaten.
        [x] The direction of energy flow. | Correct! Arrows point in the direction energy travels.
        [ ] The order in which organisms were discovered. | Incorrect. Arrows show energy flow.

    .. multichoice::

        What is an apex predator?

        [ ] An organism that feeds directly on producers. | Incorrect. That describes a primary consumer.
        [ ] An organism that makes its own food. | Incorrect. That describes a producer.
        [ ] An organism that feeds on primary consumers. | Incorrect. That describes a secondary consumer.
        [x] An animal at the top of a food chain with no natural predators. | Correct! Apex predators are at the top.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a trophic level?

        [x] The step or position an organism occupies in a food sequence. | Correct! Each step in a food chain is a trophic level.
        [ ] The total number of organisms in an ecosystem. | Incorrect. That is a population count.
        [ ] The amount of sunlight a producer receives. | Incorrect. Trophic levels describe feeding positions.
        [ ] A network of food chains. | Incorrect. That describes a food web.

    .. multichoice::

        Approximately how much energy is passed on to the next trophic level?

        [ ] 100% | Incorrect. Much energy is lost as heat and waste.
        [ ] 50% | Incorrect. Far less than half the energy is passed on.
        [x] 10% | Correct! About 10% of energy passes to the next level.
        [ ] 0% | Incorrect. Some energy is always passed on.

    .. multichoice::

        Why do higher trophic levels support fewer individuals?

        [ ] Because higher animals are always smaller. | Incorrect. Size is not the reason.
        [x] Because energy is used or lost as heat and waste at each level. | Correct! Less energy is available higher up.
        [ ] Because producers are at the top. | Incorrect. Producers are at the bottom.
        [ ] Because predators do not need energy. | Incorrect. All organisms need energy.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a primary consumer?

        [ ] An organism that feeds on secondary consumers. | Incorrect. That is a higher-level consumer.
        [ ] An organism that makes food from sunlight. | Incorrect. That describes a producer.
        [ ] An animal with no natural predators. | Incorrect. That describes an apex predator.
        [x] An organism that feeds directly on producers. | Correct! Primary consumers are herbivores.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a keystone species?

        [x] A species that has an unusually large effect on the balance of its ecosystem. | Correct! Changing its population affects the whole ecosystem.
        [ ] The smallest species in an ecosystem. | Incorrect. Keystone species are defined by their effect, not their size.
        [ ] A species found in only one food chain. | Incorrect. Keystone species affect many trophic levels.
        [ ] A species that has become extinct. | Incorrect. Keystone species are living members of an ecosystem.

    .. multichoice::

        What is a trophic cascade?

        [ ] A single pathway of energy in a food chain. | Incorrect. That describes a food chain.
        [ ] The transfer of 10% of energy between levels. | Incorrect. That describes energy transfer.
        [x] Side-effects that ripple through all trophic levels when a top organism is added or removed. | Correct! Changes to key species can shift the whole ecosystem.
        [ ] The process of producers making sugar. | Incorrect. That is photosynthesis.
