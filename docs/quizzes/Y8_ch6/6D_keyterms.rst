============================================================
6D: Everyday Energy Efficiency Key Terms
============================================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Energy star rating labels@@: Show how energy efficient an appliance is compared to other models.
    2. @@Yearly energy consumption@@: Measured in kilowatt-hours and shows total electricity used per year.
    3. @@Household power@@: The rate of energy transformed by an electrical device and is measured in watts.
    4. @@Running costs@@: Of appliances can be calculated by multiplying energy used by the electricity unit cost.

----


Summary of Key Concepts 1b
============================

.. cloze::

    1. Energy star rating labels show how @@energy efficient@@ an appliance is, while consumption is measured in @@kilowatt-hours@@.
    2. Household @@power@@ is the rate of energy transformed by an electrical device and is measured in @@watts@@.
    3. Running costs of appliances can be calculated by multiplying @@energy@@ used by the electricity unit @@cost@@.

----

Summary of Key Concepts 2
=========================

.. cloze::

    1. @@Modern appliances@@: Use newer technologies and better insulation to reduce wasted energy.
    2. @@Passive solar building design@@: Uses sunlight and natural air movement to warm and cool homes.
    3. @@Thermal mass materials@@: Like concrete and mudbrick absorb heat during the day and release it slowly.
    4. @@Household energy audit@@: Helps identify areas where energy waste can be reduced.

----

Summary of Key Concepts 2b
=============================

.. cloze::

    1. Modern appliances use @@insulation@@ to trap heat.
    2. @@Passive solar@@ design uses natural sunlight for temperature control.
    3. @@Thermal mass@@ materials absorb heat slowly.
    4. Running an @@energy audit@@ helps reduce household energy waste.
