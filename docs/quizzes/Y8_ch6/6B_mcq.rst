    .. multichoice::

        What does the Law of Conservation of Energy state?

        [x] Energy cannot be created nor destroyed. | Correct! The law states that energy can only be transferred or transformed, not created or destroyed.
        [ ] Energy is constantly destroyed when machines produce heat. | Incorrect. Heat is wasted energy, but it is not destroyed.
        [ ] Energy can be created out of nothing in a high-efficiency system. | Incorrect. Energy cannot be created; input energy always equals output energy.
        [ ] Energy increases as it changes from one form to another. | Incorrect. The total amount of energy remains constant.

    .. multichoice::

        What occurs during an energy transfer?

        [ ] Energy changes from one form into a different form. | Incorrect. Changing forms is an energy transformation.
        [x] Energy moves from one object to another in the same form. | Correct! An energy transfer keeps energy in the same form as it moves between objects.
        [ ] Energy is destroyed as heat and sound. | Incorrect. Energy is never destroyed.
        [ ] Input energy becomes larger than output energy. | Incorrect. Total energy is always conserved.

    .. multichoice::

        What occurs during an energy transformation?

        [ ] Energy moves between two objects without changing form. | Incorrect. Moving without changing form is an energy transfer.
        [x] Energy changes from one form to another inside an object. | Correct! An energy transformation involves changing from one form of energy to another.
        [ ] Useful output energy vanishes from the system. | Incorrect. Energy cannot vanish or be destroyed.
        [ ] Wasted energy is converted back into input energy automatically. | Incorrect. Wasted energy is output energy that spreads into the surroundings.

    .. multichoice::

        In a hairdryer, what is the primary input energy?

        [ ] Thermal energy | Incorrect. Heat is an output energy produced by the hairdryer.
        [ ] Sound energy | Incorrect. Sound is an unwanted output energy.
        [x] Electrical energy | Correct! Electrical energy is the input energy supplied to power the hairdryer.
        [ ] Chemical energy | Incorrect. Hairdryers use electricity from an outlet rather than chemical energy.

    .. multichoice::

        In a mobile phone, chemical energy stored in the battery is first transformed into:

        [x] Electrical energy | Correct! Chemical energy in the battery is transformed into electrical energy to power the phone's circuits.
        [ ] Gravitational potential energy | Incorrect. Gravitational potential energy depends on the phone's height off the ground.
        [ ] Elastic energy | Incorrect. Elastic energy is stored in stretched or compressed materials.
        [ ] Nuclear energy | Incorrect. Mobile phones do not use nuclear reactions.

    .. multichoice::

        What type of diagram visually represents the amounts of useful energy and wasted energy in a system using proportional arrows?

        [ ] Venn diagram | Incorrect. Venn diagrams show relationships between sets, not energy flows.
        [ ] Scatter plot | Incorrect. Scatter plots display relationships between two variables.
        [x] Sankey diagram | Correct! Sankey diagrams visually represent useful and wasted energy flows using scaled arrows.
        [ ] Cloze diagram | Incorrect. Cloze refers to fill-in-the-blank text exercises.

    .. multichoice::

        According to energy conservation principles, how does total input energy compare to total output energy in a closed system?

        [ ] Input energy is always greater than total output energy. | Incorrect. Energy cannot be lost or destroyed, so total input equals total output.
        [ ] Output energy is always greater than total input energy. | Incorrect. Energy cannot be created out of nothing.
        [x] Total input energy entering a system is always equal to total output energy coming out. | Correct! The total input energy always equals the sum of all output energy (useful + wasted).
        [ ] Input and output energy have no fixed relationship. | Incorrect. They are always strictly equal due to the law of conservation of energy.

    .. multichoice::

        How do flow diagrams illustrate energy changes in a system?

        [ ] They use pie charts to show mass. | Incorrect. Flow diagrams use arrows to depict energy sequences.
        [x] They use arrows to show input energy changing into output energy. | Correct! Flow diagrams use directional arrows to map energy transformations and transfers.
        [ ] They use line graphs to plot temperature over time. | Incorrect. Line graphs track continuous data, not energy transformation steps.
        [ ] They list chemical formulas of reactants. | Incorrect. Flow diagrams track energy forms, not chemical formulas.

    .. multichoice::

        What is waste energy?

        [ ] Energy that is created during a reaction and destroyed afterward. | Incorrect. Energy cannot be created or destroyed.
        [ ] Energy that is stored in batteries for future use. | Incorrect. Stored energy in batteries is chemical potential energy.
        [x] Energy that is changed into unwanted forms like excess heat or sound. | Correct! Waste energy is output energy that does not serve the intended function of the device.
        [ ] The total starting energy put into an object. | Incorrect. Starting energy put into a system is input energy.

    .. multichoice::

        Which of the following is the best example of energy transformation in a hairdryer?

        [ ] Kinetic energy transferring from the wire into the wall socket. | Incorrect. Electricity travels through wires as electrical energy.
        [x] Electrical energy transforming into heat, kinetic, and sound energy. | Correct! Electrical input energy turns into thermal (heat), kinetic (fan moving), and sound energy.
        [ ] Thermal energy turning into chemical energy in the motor. | Incorrect. Hairdryers convert electrical energy into heat and motion, not chemical energy.
        [ ] Light energy transforming into gravitational potential energy. | Incorrect. Hairdryers do not operate on light energy.
