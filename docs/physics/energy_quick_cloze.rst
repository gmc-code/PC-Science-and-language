====================================================
Energy Quick Cloze
====================================================

.. cloze::

    #. The ability to make changes to our world is called @@energy@@.
    #. Energy stored due to an object's position or state is called @@potential energy@@.
    #. The energy an object has due to being in motion is @@kinetic energy@@.
    #. The energy carried by charged particles is called @@electrical energy@@.
    #. The energy carried by sound waves is called @@sound energy@@.


.. cloze::

    #. The energy carried by tiny particles called photons is @@light energy@@.
    #. The form of kinetic energy that moving particles within a substance have is @@thermal energy@@.
    #. Thermal energy being transferred between objects is called @@heat energy@@.
    #. A measure of the average kinetic energy of particles in a substance is @@temperature@@.
    #. Stored energy when an object is raised above the ground is @@gravitational potential energy@@.


.. cloze::

    #. The force that pulls objects towards Earth is @@gravity@@.
    #. The scientist inspired by a falling apple to study gravity was @@Isaac Newton@@.
    #. Energy found in the bonds of compounds and molecules is @@chemical energy@@.
    #. An everyday source of chemical energy is @@food@@.
    #. Energy stored in materials that stretch, bounce, or spring back is @@elastic energy@@.


.. cloze::

    #. Squeezing or pressing a material together is called @@compressing@@.
    #. Energy released when atoms are combined or split apart is @@nuclear energy@@.
    #. The celestial object that combines atoms to release heat and light is @@the Sun@@.
    #. Charged particles built up on a surface until released is called @@static electricity@@.
    #. The back-and-forth motion of particles is described as @@vibrations@@.


.. cloze::

    #. Radiation that humans can sense as heat but cannot see is @@infrared@@.
    #. Light energy is classified as @@neither@@ potential nor kinetic energy.
    #. The two main categories of energy are @@potential and kinetic@@.
    #. When a raised object falls, its gravitational potential energy transforms into @@kinetic energy@@.
    #. Heat energy always transfers from @@hot to cold@@.


.. cloze::

    #. Both a battery and a banana contain @@chemical potential energy@@.
    #. In a video game console, electrical energy changes to @@sound and light@@.
    #. On a roller coaster, potential energy is at its highest at the @@highest point@@.
    #. On a roller coaster, kinetic energy is at its highest at the @@lowest point@@.
    #. As kinetic energy increases in a system, potential energy @@decreases@@.
