Background: resistance and Ohm's law
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Key ideas
    :class: note

    * **Resistance (R)** is a measure of how difficult it is for electrons to flow through part of an electric circuit.
    * Resistance is measured in **ohms (Ω)**.
    * **Ohm's law**: V = I × R, so **R = V ÷ I** (resistance = voltage ÷ current).
    * **V** is the voltage in volts (V). **I** is the electric current in amps (A).
    * **High resistance**: it is very difficult for electrons to move through.
    * **Low resistance**: it is easy for electrons to move through.

.. admonition:: In this investigation
    :class: tip

    | **Independent variable**: the number of light globes connected in series (1, 2 or 3).
    | **Dependent variable**: the resistance of the circuit, calculated using Ohm's law.
