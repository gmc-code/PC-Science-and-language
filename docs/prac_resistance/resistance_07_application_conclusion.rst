Part C: Analysis and Evaluation

Question 14: Callum's electric car
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Scenario
    :class: tip

    Callum is a car mechanic working on a new electric car model. Wires are used to connect the motor to the battery
    that stores electric charge for the car. Thin wires have more resistance than thick wires. The wires connecting
    the battery to the motor are thick because the current from the battery is large.

.. admonition:: Key ideas
    :class: note

    The resistance of a wire depends on:

    * **thickness**: a thin wire has **more** resistance than a thick wire
    * **length**: a long wire has **more** resistance than a short wire
    * **material**: a good conductor such as copper has **less** resistance
    * **temperature**: a hot wire has **more** resistance than a cool wire

    From Ohm's law, I = V ÷ R. With the same voltage, **more resistance** means **less current**.

Question 14a: Thin wires
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. admonition:: Question 14a
    :class: important

    Research how changing to thin wires to connect the battery to the motor would impact the resistance of the circuit,
    and hence the effect on the motor output.

.. cloze::
    :instructions: Drag each word from the word bank into the correct gap. Each word is used once.

    1. Thin wires have @@more@@ resistance than thick wires because there is less space for the electrons to flow through.
    2. Changing to thin wires would therefore @@increase@@ the resistance of the circuit.
    3. With the same battery voltage, a higher resistance means @@less@@ current reaches the motor.
    4. The motor would therefore have a lower power @@output@@, so the car would be slower.
    5. The thin wires would also get @@hot@@, so some electrical energy would be wasted as @@heat@@.

Question 14b: Another way to reduce resistance
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

.. admonition:: Question 14b
    :class: important

    Investigating other factors that impact resistance, suggest another way that Callum could reduce the resistance
    in the wires connecting the motor to the battery. In your answer explain why this change would reduce the resistance.

.. admonition:: Watch out
    :class: warning

    The question says **another** way, so do not write about the thickness of the wires again.
    One mark is for the change and one mark is for **why** it reduces resistance.

.. cloze::
    :instructions: Drag each word from the word bank into the correct gap. Each word is used once.

    1. Callum could use @@shorter@@ wires to connect the motor to the battery.
    2. In a shorter wire, the electrons travel a smaller @@distance@@.
    3. The electrons have fewer @@collisions@@ with the atoms in the wire.
    4. This makes it @@easier@@ for the electrons to flow, so the resistance is @@lower@@.
    5. Callum could also choose wires made from a better @@conductor@@, such as copper.

Question 15: Conclusion
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Question 15
    :class: important

    Write a conclusion answering the research question using the results.

    * Refer to your aim and hypothesis from Part A.
    * Summarise the key findings of the experiment with reference to at least one numerical value.
    * Acknowledge one limitation or improvement of the investigation.

.. admonition:: Key ideas
    :class: note

    A conclusion has three parts, one mark each:

    1. **Aim and hypothesis**: restate the aim and say whether the hypothesis was **supported** or **not supported**.
    2. **Key findings**: describe the trend and quote **data with units**.
    3. **Limitation or improvement**: name one and say how it would help.

.. admonition:: Watch out
    :class: warning

    Write that a hypothesis was **supported**, never that it was "proven" or "correct".
    Do not introduce new results in a conclusion.
