The Globes in Series Investigation
------------------------------------

.. admonition:: About this model investigation
    :class: note

    The Electrical Circuits Practical Inquiry Task asks you to investigate a factor that affects **resistance**.
    These pages work through the whole task, step by step, for one choice of independent variable:
    the **number of light globes connected in series**.

    The task has three parts:

    * **Part A: Practical Design** (materials, variables, aim, hypothesis, safety, circuit diagram, method)
    * **Part B: Collection of Data** (results table, calculating resistance, graph)
    * **Part C: Analysis and Evaluation** (trends, theory, precision, errors, application, conclusion)

    Your own investigation may use a different independent variable, but the way each answer is built is the same.

How to use these pages
-------------------------------

| Work through the pages in order. Each page covers a few questions from the task.
| For each question:
| 1. Read the **question** and the **key ideas**.
| 2. **Fill in the gaps**: drag each word from the word bank into the correct gap to build a model answer. Each word is used once.
| 3. Press **Check**, then fix any gaps that are wrong.

| How to read the boxes:
| **Question** — the question from the task booklet.
| **Key ideas** — the science and skills you need to know.
| **Watch out** — common ways to lose marks.
