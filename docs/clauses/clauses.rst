========================================
Clauses
========================================

| A clause is the basic unit of meaning.
| A major clause is all about representing a process (an action, event, or state) unfolding in time, involving participants, and happening under specific circumstances.
| A major clause is a clause containing a finite verb (anchored in time/person) and a Mood structure (Subject + Finite, allowing the clause to declare, ask, or command).


.. list-table:: Major Clause Types in Year 7 Science
   :header-rows: 1
   :stub-columns: 1
   :widths: 25 35 40

   * - Major Clause Type (Mood)
     - Lab / Science Purpose
     - Year 7 Science Example
   * - Declarative
     - Stating facts, observations, or explanations.
     - *Magnesium reacts with acid.*
   * - Imperative
     - Giving lab instructions, commands, or steps.
     - *Measure 50 mL of water.* / *Observe!*
   * - Interrogative
     - Asking questions, posing hypotheses, or testing ideas.
     - *Does temperature affect the rate of reaction?*
   * - Exclamatory
     - Expressing strong reactions or sudden findings.
     - *How fast that reacted!* / *What a rapid change!*
