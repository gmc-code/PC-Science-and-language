========================================
Independent Clauses
========================================

*These are complete scientific statements that can stand alone.*

* **Biology:** The cell membrane :process:`controls` the movement of substances into and out of the cell.
* **Chemistry:** Sodium chloride :process:`dissolves` in water at room temperature.
* **Physics:** Gravity :process:`pulls` objects toward the center of the Earth.
* **Earth Science:** Convection currents in the mantle :process:`drive` the movement of tectonic plates.

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Process (action verb).
    :color: green

    Enzymes {{catalyse}} chemical reactions in living organisms.
    Exothermic reactions {{emit}} heat into the surroundings.
    Light waves {{cross}} space without a medium.
    Weathering {{erodes}} rocks into smaller sediments.


Independent clause patterns
---------------------------


1. Participant + Process
------------------------

* Structure: **Participant + Process**
* Example: *The sun shines.*

1. Participant + Process + Circumstance
----------------------------------------

* Structure: **Participant + Process + Circumstance**
* Example: *Light travels through a vacuum.*

1. Participant + Process + Participant
----------------------------------------

* Structure: **Participant + Process + Participant**
* Example: *Acids neutralize bases.*

1. Participant + Process + Participant + Circumstance
-----------------------------------------------------

* Structure: **Participant + Process + Participant + Circumstance**
* Example: *Gravity pulls objects toward the center of the Earth.*

1. Circumstance + Participant + Process + Participant
-----------------------------------------------------

* Structure: **Circumstance + Participant + Process + Participant**
* Example: *In the mantle, convection currents drive tectonic plates.*

1. Circumstance + Participant + Process
---------------------------------------

* Structure: **Circumstance + Participant + Process**
* Example: *At room temperature, ice melts.*

1. Participant + Process + Circumstance + Circumstance
------------------------------------------------------

* Structure: **Participant + Process + Circumstance + Circumstance**
* Example: *Sediments settle in layers on the ocean floor.*

1. Participant + Participant + Process
--------------------------------------

* Structure: **Participant + Participant + Process**
* Example: *Chloroplasts sunlight absorb.* (Note: English rarely uses this structure, but in specialized labels: *Cells oxygen absorb.*)
