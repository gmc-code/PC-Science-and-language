===================================================
3E The Impact of Invasive Species
===================================================

Ecological Concepts and Definitions
===================================================

* Introduced species are organisms taken outside their natural geographical area, while invasive species spread quickly and cause significant environmental or economic harm.
* Ecosystems experience population declines or localized extinctions when invasive species outcompete native species for limited resources like food, water, and shelter.
* Introduced species enter new environments through deliberate pathways (such as agriculture, pest control, and transport) or accidental pathways (such as cargo shipping and ballast water).

* **Key Terms:**

    * **Species**: A group of closely related organisms capable of interbreeding.
    * **Native Species**: Organisms that occur naturally in a specific ecological region.
    * **Introduced Species**: Organisms transported beyond their natural geographical boundary by human activity.
    * **Invasive Species**: An introduced species that spreads rapidly and causes harm to native species or ecosystems.
    * **Eradication**: The complete removal or destruction of a target species from an area.

.. cloze::

    1. Organisms that occur naturally in a region are described as @@native@@ species.
    2. An introduced organism that spreads rapidly and causes environmental harm is an @@invasive@@ species.
    3. The complete removal of a species from a targeted geographic area is called @@eradication@@.

----

Mechanisms of Introduction and Ecological Impact
===================================================

* Deliberate introductions occur for farming, pest control, hunting, or transport, whereas accidental introductions occur via shipping containers and vessel ballast water.
* Broad generalist predators alter food webs across multiple trophic levels by consuming native prey and competing directly with endemic predators.
* Invasive species like the cane toad disrupt multiple trophic levels simultaneously, impacting algae as tadpoles, consuming native insects as adults, and poisoning native predators via skin toxins.

* **Key Terms:**

    * **Biological Control**: The intentional release of a natural enemy or pathogen to manage a pest population.
    * **Trophic Level**: The position an organism occupies in a food chain or food web.
    * **Resource Competition**: The struggle between organisms for shared, limited resources such as food, shelter, or territory.

.. cloze::

    1. Organisms brought into new habitats via cargo ships or ballast water are examples of @@accidental@@ introductions.
    2. Invasive species disrupt ecosystems by engaging in direct @@competition@@ with native organisms for limited resources.
    3. Using a pathogen or natural predator to control a pest population is known as @@biological@@ control.
