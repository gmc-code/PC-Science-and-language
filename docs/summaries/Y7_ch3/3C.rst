==================================
3C The Role of Microorganisms
==================================

Understanding Microorganisms
=============================

* Microorganisms (microbes) are organisms too small to be seen without a microscope.
* Microbes originate from three main kingdoms of life: Monera, Fungi, and Protista.
* Microorganisms fulfil three critical ecological roles across ecosystems: producers, consumers, and decomposers.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Microorganism**: An organism that requires a microscope to be seen.
  * **Microscopic**: Too small to be seen without the aid of a microscope.
  * **Monera**: A kingdom of unicellular prokaryotic organisms, such as bacteria.
  * **Protista**: A diverse kingdom of eukaryotic microorganisms that do not fit into other kingdoms.

    1. Organisms that require a microscope to be seen are known as @@microorganisms@@.
    2. Disease-causing bacteria like *Salmonella* belong to the kingdom @@Monera@@.
    3. Microorganisms act as producers, consumers, or @@decomposers@@ in ecosystems.

----

Microbial Producers and Consumers
=================================

* Photosynthetic microorganisms such as phytoplankton and green algae act as primary producers, forming the foundation of aquatic trophic pyramids.
* Microbial consumers (heterotrophs) obtain energy by consuming other microorganisms or organic matter.
* While microbes can occupy lower trophic levels like producers or primary consumers, many also live inside larger animals to aid digestion.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Producer**: An organism that creates its own energy via processes like photosynthesis.
  * **Heterotroph**: An organism that obtains energy by consuming other living or dead organisms.
  * **Phytoplankton**: Microscopic photosynthetic organisms in oceans and fresh water that form the base of aquatic food webs.

.. cloze::

    1. Green algae act as primary @@producers@@ in marine food webs by capturing solar energy.
    2. Microorganisms that eat other microbes for energy are classified as @@heterotrophs@@.
    3. Microscopic organisms generally occupy the first few @@trophic levels@@ of a food chain.

----

Microbial Decomposers and Nutrient Cycling
==========================================

* Microbial decomposers, primarily fungi and bacteria, break down detritus (dead matter and waste) externally and absorb necessary nutrients.
* Decomposers process organic matter from every trophic level, recycling essential minerals back into the soil or aquatic environment as natural fertiliser.
* Without decomposers, organic waste would accumulate, blocking sunlight and air from reaching soil, ultimately stopping plant growth and causing ecosystem collapse.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Detritus**: Waste matter in an ecosystem, including dead organisms and faeces.
  * **Decomposer**: An organism that breaks down dead organic material externally to recycle nutrients.
  * **Nutrient Cycling**: The recycling of nutrients from dead matter back into soil and water for producers to reuse.

.. cloze::

    1. Matter composed of dead organisms and faeces is referred to as @@detritus@@.
    2. Decomposers break down organic matter and return nutrients to the @@soil@@ for producers to use.
    3. Energy flows to decomposers from @@all@@ trophic levels when organisms die or excrete waste.
