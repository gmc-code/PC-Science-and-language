3A: Nature's Pantry

Ecosystems and Environmental Factors
==============================================

* Every living organism lives within an ecosystem where living and non-living elements interact.
* Ecosystems can be aquatic (freshwater, marine/coastal) or terrestrial (desert, jungle, tundra, grassland).
* Biotic factors are all the living components present in an ecosystem, such as animals, plants, and bacteria.
* Abiotic factors are the non-living physical components, such as sunlight, temperature, water, oxygen, and shelter.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Organism**: An individual living thing.
  * **Ecosystem**: An environment where living organisms interact with each other and non-living elements.
  * **Biotic Factor**: Any living organism or biological feature within an ecosystem.
  * **Abiotic Factor**: Any non-living physical or chemical factor in an ecosystem.


.. cloze::

    1. A polar bear relies on abiotic factors like ice and @@temperature@@ to survive in the @@tundra@@.
    2. Living components like plants are @@biotic@@ factors, while non-living items like sunlight are @@abiotic@@ factors.
    3. An @@ecosystem@@ contains both living and non-living factors that interact with every @@organism@@.

Energy Flow and Trophic Roles
==============================================

* Autotrophs (producers) convert energy from sunlight to create their own food through photosynthesis.
* Heterotrophs (consumers) must consume other organisms to gain energy and nutrients.
* Consumers are classified by diet: herbivores eat plants, carnivores eat meat, and omnivores eat both plants and meat.
* Earth's cleanup crew includes scavengers, detritivores, and decomposers, which break down dead matter and waste.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Autotroph**: An organism that makes its own food using light energy from the Sun (producer).
  * **Heterotroph**: An organism that obtains energy by consuming other living or dead organisms (consumer).
  * **Herbivore**: An organism that eats only plants.
  * **Carnivore**: An organism that eats only meat.
  * **Omnivore**: An organism that eats both plants and animals.
  * **Decomposer**: An organism that breaks down dead organic material using external chemical digestion.


.. cloze::

    1. Plants are @@autotroph@@ organisms that use @@sunlight@@ to make energy.
    2. A lion is a @@carnivore@@ because it only eats meat, while a goat is an @@herbivore@@.
    3. Fungi act as a @@decomposer@@ by using external digestion to break down @@dead@@ matter.

Ecosystem Interactions
=======================

* Organisms compete with each other when vital resources such as food, water, light, space, or mates are limited.
* Competition negatively affects both competing individuals because both must expend extra energy.
* Predator-prey interactions keep population sizes balanced within an ecosystem over time.
* Changes in abiotic conditions or predator numbers directly impact the survival rates of prey species.


* **Key Terms:**

  * **Resource**: Anything in the environment required by an organism for survival.
  * **Competition**: An interaction where organisms fight for the same limited resources.
  * **Predator**: An organism that hunts, kills, and eats other organisms for food.
  * **Prey**: An organism that is hunted and eaten by a predator.


.. cloze::

    1. When resources are limited, @@competition@@ increases and affects an organism's ability to @@survive@@.
    2. A @@predator@@ hunts and eats another organism, which is known as the @@prey@@.
    3. An increase in prey population leads to more food for predators to @@reproduce@@ and @@grow@@.
