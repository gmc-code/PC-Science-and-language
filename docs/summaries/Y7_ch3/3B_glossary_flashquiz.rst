   # 3B Food chains and food webs
   An animal at the top of a food chain or web that has no natural predators | apex predator
   Has a wide variety of organisms within the ecosystem | biodiverse
   A pathway showing the flow of energy through feeding relationships | food chain
   A diagram showing multiple feeding relationships in an ecosystem | food web
   A species that is integral to the health of an ecosystem | keystone species
   Produces its own food; an autotroph | producer
   The addition or removal of an organism that affects all trophic levels | trophic cascade
   An organism that eats other organisms to obtain energy | consumer
   An organism that gets energy by breaking down the dead remains of organisms | decomposer
   The movement of energy from one organism to the next in a food chain or web | energy flow
   Energy that does not pass to the next trophic level because it is used to grow, move, breathe and keep warm, or lost as waste | energy loss
   The link between an organism and what it eats | feeding relationship
   A model showing the amount of energy at each trophic level | food pyramid
   A unit used to measure energy | kilojoule
   An animal that kills and eats other animals | predator
   An animal that is killed and eaten by a predator | prey
   The first consumer in a food chain; it eats the producer | primary consumer
   The consumer that eats the primary consumer | secondary consumer
   The consumer that eats the secondary consumer; the fourth trophic level | tertiary consumer
   The position of an organism in a food chain (from the Greek trophe, meaning nourishment) | trophic level
