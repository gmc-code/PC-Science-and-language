====================================
7B: Series and Parallel Circuits
====================================

Summary of Key Concepts
----------------------------------------

* Current only flows in a closed circuit with an unbroken path.
* A conductor is a material that allows electric current to flow through it easily.
* The simplest electric circuit contains a power supply, connecting wires, and a load.
* A power supply, such as a battery, provides the voltage that pushes current around a circuit.
* Voltage is the electrical push that moves energy, and it is measured in volts using a voltmeter.
* A voltmeter is connected in parallel, across a component.
* Current is the flow of electric charge, and it is measured in amps using an ammeter.
* An ammeter is connected in series with the components.
* Resistance opposes the flow of electric current, reducing the current in a circuit.
* Opening a switch breaks the circuit loop and causes all electrical current to stop.
* A multimeter can measure voltage, current, and resistance.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Conductor**: A material that lets current flow easily.
  * **Load**: A component that transforms electrical energy into other forms.
  * **Voltage**: The electrical push, measured in volts.
  * **Current**: The flow of electric charge, measured in amps.
  * **Resistance**: How much a component reduces current.
