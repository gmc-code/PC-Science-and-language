Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy by-products are secondary forms of energy produced during energy transfers or transformations.
* Input energy is the total amount of energy going into an energy transfer or transformation system.
* Output energy is the total energy coming out of an energy transfer or transformation system.
* Useful energy is output energy that is needed and used by us for a specific purpose.
* Waste energy is output energy that is not useful to us, usually released as heat, sound, or light.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Efficient means maximising useful output energy while creating the smallest possible amount of waste energy.
* Conduction transfers thermal energy through direct contact between particles in a solid, liquid, or gas.
* Convection transfers heat through the movement of liquids or gases, while radiation transfers heat via electromagnetic waves.
