================================================
6B Energy Transfers & Transformations
================================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* The law of conservation of energy states that energy cannot be created nor destroyed.
* During an energy transfer, energy moves from one object to another in the same form.
* During an energy transformation, energy changes from one form to another inside an object.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Flow diagrams use arrows to show input energy changing into output energy.
* In a hairdryer, electrical energy is transformed into heat, kinetic, and sound energy.
* In a mobile phone, chemical energy from the battery is transformed into electrical energy.
* Sankey diagrams visually show the amount of useful energy and wasted energy in a system.
* Total input energy entering a system is always equal to the total output energy coming out.
