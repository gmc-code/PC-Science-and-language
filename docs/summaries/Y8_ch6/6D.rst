============================================
6D: Everyday Energy Efficiency
============================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy star rating labels show how energy efficient an appliance is compared to other models.
* Yearly energy consumption is measured in kilowatt-hours and shows total electricity used per year.
* Household power is the rate of energy transformed by an electrical device and is measured in watts.
* Running costs of appliances can be calculated by multiplying energy used by the electricity unit cost.

 Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Modern appliances use newer technologies and better insulation to reduce wasted energy.
* Passive solar building design uses sunlight and natural air movement to warm and cool homes.
* Thermal mass materials like concrete and mudbrick absorb heat during the day and release it slowly.
* Conducting a household energy audit helps identify areas where energy waste can be reduced.

Matching Exercise
----------------------------------------

.. cloze::

   * @@Energy Audit@@ -> An official inspection of a home to check energy usage and reduce waste.
   * @@Thermal Mass@@ -> Materials like concrete or brick that absorb, store, and release heat energy slowly.
   * @@Passive Solar Design@@ -> Building design using natural climate and sunlight to heat/cool without power.
   * @@Insulation@@ -> Material used to trap air and prevent unwanted heat loss or gain.
   * @@Running Cost@@ -> The total amount of money spent to operate an electrical appliance over time.
   * @@Power@@ -> The rate at which energy is transformed or used by a device, measured in watts.
   * @@Kilowatt-hour (kWh)@@ -> The unit of energy used to measure home electricity consumption over time.
   * @@Energy Star Rating@@ -> Label showing how efficient an appliance is compared to similar models.
