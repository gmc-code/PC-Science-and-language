============================================
6D: Everyday Energy Efficiency
============================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy Rating Labels use stars to show how energy efficient an appliance is compared to other models; more stars means more efficient.
* Yearly electricity use is measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
* Power is the rate at which an electrical device transforms energy, measured in watts (1 watt = 1 joule per second).
* Running cost = energy used (kWh) × price per kWh; for example, 2 kWh × 30c = 60c.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Energy Rating Label**: A star label comparing appliance efficiency.
  * **Kilowatt-hour (kWh)**: The unit for measuring household electricity use.
  * **Power**: The rate a device transforms energy, measured in watts.
  * **Running Cost**: Money spent operating an appliance over time.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Modern appliances use efficient technology, such as LED lighting, and better insulation to reduce wasted energy.
* Passive solar building design uses sunlight and natural air movement to warm and cool homes, with features such as north-facing windows and wide eaves.
* Thermal mass materials like concrete and mudbrick absorb heat during the day and release it slowly.
* Conducting a household energy audit helps identify areas where energy waste can be reduced.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Insulation**: Material that traps air to reduce heat flow.
  * **Passive Solar Design**: Using sunlight and airflow to heat and cool homes.
  * **Thermal Mass**: Materials that absorb heat and release it slowly.
  * **Energy Audit**: An inspection of a home's energy use.
