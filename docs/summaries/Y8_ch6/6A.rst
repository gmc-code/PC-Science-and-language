================================================
6A Forms of Energy
================================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy is the ability to make changes, move objects, or do work.
* Potential energy is stored energy that an object has due to its position or state.
* Kinetic energy is the energy of a moving object.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Energy**: The ability to make changes or do work.
  * **Potential Energy**: Stored energy due to position or state.
  * **Kinetic Energy**: The energy of a moving object.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Thermal energy is the energy of moving particles; particles in hotter objects move faster.
* Chemical energy is stored in the chemical bonds of food, fuel, and batteries.
* Gravitational potential energy is stored when an object is raised high above the ground.
* Elastic energy is stored when materials like springs or rubber bands are stretched or squeezed.
* Sound energy is carried by vibrating particles, such as air, to reach our ears.
* Electrical energy is carried by moving charged particles, such as electrons, and powers devices.
* Radiant energy, such as light and infrared, travels as electromagnetic waves and can cross empty space.
* Nuclear energy is released when the nuclei of atoms are split or combined.
* Mechanical energy is the energy of moving parts in machines.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Thermal Energy**: The energy of moving particles in a substance.
  * **Chemical Energy**: Energy stored in chemical bonds.
  * **Gravitational Energy**: Stored energy of objects raised above the ground.
  * **Elastic Energy**: Energy stored in stretched or squeezed materials.
  * **Radiant Energy**: Energy carried by electromagnetic waves.
  * **Nuclear Energy**: Energy released from the nuclei of atoms.
