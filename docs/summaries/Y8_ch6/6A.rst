================================================
6A Forms of Energy
================================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy is the ability to make changes, move objects, or do work.
* Potential energy is stored energy that an object has due to its position or state.
* Kinetic energy is the energy of a moving object.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Thermal energy is the energy of moving particles inside a hot object.
* Chemical energy is stored in the bonds of food, fuel, and batteries.
* Gravitational potential energy is stored when an object is raised high above the ground.
* Elastic energy is stored when materials like springs or rubber bands are stretched or squeezed.
* Sound energy moves through vibrating particles of air to reach our ears.
