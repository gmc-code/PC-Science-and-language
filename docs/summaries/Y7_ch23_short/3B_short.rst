===============================
3B Food Chains and Food Webs
===============================

Understanding Food Chains
=========================

* A food chain shows who eats who.
* It shows a single pathway of energy.
* Every food chain begins with a producer.
* Producers capture sunlight to make sugar.
* Each step is called a trophic level.
* Levels move from producers to consumers.
* The apex predator sits at the top.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Food Chain**: A sequence showing energy passing through feeding.
  * **Producer**: An autotroph converting sunlight into food.
  * **Apex Predator**: A top animal with no natural predators.
  * **Trophic Level**: An organism's position in a food sequence.

.. cloze::

    1. Energy enters when a @@producer@@ uses sunlight.
    2. Food chain arrows show the energy @@flow@@.
    3. A top animal is the @@apex predator@@.

----

Energy Transfer and Trophic Pyramids
====================================

* Energy is lost at each trophic level.
* It is used or lost as heat.
* Waste also removes energy from the chain.
* Only about 10% passes to the next level.
* Trophic pyramids show this energy reduction.
* Higher levels support fewer individual organisms.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Trophic Pyramid**: A diagram showing energy decreasing up levels.
  * **Primary Consumer**: A herbivore feeding directly on producers.
  * **Secondary Consumer**: An organism that feeds on primary consumers.

.. cloze::

    1. Only about @@10%@@ of energy is transferred.
    2. Growth, movement and @@waste@@ use up energy.
    3. Higher levels support a smaller @@population@@.

----

Food Webs and Ecosystem Dynamics
================================

* Food webs combine many interconnected food chains.
* They model complex feeding relationships.
* Keystone species keep ecosystems stable.
* Changing their populations affects all levels.
* This can trigger a trophic cascade.
* Predators, prey, and producers become unbalanced.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Food Web**: A network of interconnected food chains.
  * **Biodiverse**: Containing many different kinds of organisms.
  * **Keystone Species**: A species with large effects on its ecosystem.
  * **Trophic Cascade**: Ripple effects through levels when top organisms change.

.. cloze::

    1. Interconnected feeding pathways form a @@food web@@.
    2. A @@keystone species@@ can alter the whole ecosystem.
    3. Changing top predators causes a @@trophic cascade@@.
