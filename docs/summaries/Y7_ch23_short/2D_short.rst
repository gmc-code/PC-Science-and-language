2D Discovering the Kingdoms
==============================

History of Classification and Microscopy
====================================================================

* Classification has changed throughout history.
* New tools and observations improved classification.
* Aristotle began early taxonomy long ago.
* Linnaeus later expanded the classification system.
* Early taxonomy only included visible organisms.
* Microscopes were invented in the 1600s.
* Microscopes revealed microscopic life and cells.
* Microscopy shows the true complexity of life.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Microscope**: An instrument used to view very small objects.
  * **Microorganisms**: Organisms needing a microscope to see, called microbes.


.. cloze::

   1. Scientific understanding grows as new @@technology@@ develops.
   2. Microscopes introduced classification to @@microscopic@@ life.
   3. Agreeing on microbe @@classification@@ took over 300 years.

The Animal Kingdom
==================================

* Animals get nutrition by eating other organisms.
* Animals are invertebrates or vertebrates.
* Vertebrates belong to the phylum Chordata.
* Vertebrates form five classes of animals.
* Classes are mammals, reptiles, fish, amphibians, birds.
* The egg-laying platypus is tricky to classify.
* Genetic material reveals deeper cellular similarities.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Phylum**: A classification rank directly below kingdom.
  * **Invertebrates**: Animals that do not have a backbone.
  * **Vertebrates**: Animals that do have a backbone.


.. cloze::

   1. All vertebrates belong to phylum @@Chordata@@.
   2. Animals without a backbone are @@invertebrates@@.
   3. Microscopic @@genetic@@ material helps classify unusual organisms.

The Plant Kingdom
==================================

* Plants make their own food from sunlight.
* Angiosperms are flowering plants with fruit-covered seeds.
* Gymnosperms produce their seeds on cones.
* Ferns and mosses reproduce using spores.
* Microscopes helped scientists study tiny spores.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Angiosperms**: Flowering plants with seeds inside protective fruit.
  * **Gymnosperms**: Non-flowering plants that produce seeds on cones.
  * **Spores**: Tiny units that develop into new individuals.
  * **Pollination**: Transfer of pollen between plants for fertilization.


.. cloze::

   1. Plants make nutrients using energy from @@sunlight@@.
   2. @@Gymnosperms@@ produce their seeds inside cones.
   3. Ferns and mosses reproduce using @@spores@@.

The Protist Kingdom
==================================

* Some microorganisms had plant and animal features.
* Kingdom Protista was created for these organisms.
* Protists include algae, protozoa and slime moulds.
* *Euglena* makes energy from sunlight like plants.
* *Euglena* also moves and eats like animals.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Protista**: A kingdom for organisms fitting nowhere else.
  * **Protozoa**: Animal-like protists that move to capture food.


.. cloze::

   1. Protists blur the line between @@plants@@ and animals.
   2. @@Protozoa@@ can move and ingest nutrients.
   3. *Euglena* is classified as a @@protist@@.

The Fungi Kingdom
==================================

* Fungi were first classified as plants.
* Fungi grow from the ground like plants.
* Fungi do not use sunlight for energy.
* Fungi absorb nutrients from decaying matter.
* Mushrooms and moulds are multicellular fungi.
* Yeasts are tiny, single-celled fungi.
* Some edible mushrooms resemble poisonous species.
* Genus-level identification is therefore critical.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Fungi**: Organisms that absorb organic matter for nutrition.
  * **Yeasts**: Single-celled fungi used in food production.


.. cloze::

   1. Fungi absorb energy from @@decaying@@ matter.
   2. @@Yeasts@@ are fungi made of single cells.
   3. Mushrooms need identification to the @@genus@@ level.

The Monera Kingdom and Three Domains
=====================================

* Microscopes showed bacteria lack a nucleus.
* Bacteria were placed in kingdom Monera.
* DNA showed Monera had two branches.
* Monera's two branches are Bacteria and Archaea.
* Bacteria live in soil and water.
* Archaea thrive in extreme places like hot springs.
* Life now has three domains.
* The domains are Bacteria, Archaea, and Eukarya.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Monera**: A historical kingdom of organisms without nuclei.
  * **Bacteria**: Single-celled microorganisms found in everyday environments.
  * **Archaea**: Single-celled organisms living in extreme conditions.


.. cloze::

   1. Bacteria cells lack a @@nucleus@@.
   2. @@Archaea@@ live in extreme environments.
   3. Life is organized into three @@domains@@.
