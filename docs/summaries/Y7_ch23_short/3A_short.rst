Ecosystems and Environmental Factors
==============================================

* Every organism lives within an ecosystem.
* Living and non-living elements interact in ecosystems.
* Aquatic ecosystems include freshwater and marine habitats.
* Terrestrial ecosystems include desert, jungle, tundra, grassland.
* Biotic factors are living parts of ecosystems.
* Biotic examples include animals, plants, and bacteria.
* Abiotic factors are non-living physical parts.
* Abiotic examples include sunlight, temperature, water, oxygen.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Organism**: An individual living thing or life form.
  * **Ecosystem**: Living organisms interacting with non-living elements.
  * **Biotic Factor**: Any living organism within an ecosystem.
  * **Abiotic Factor**: Any non-living physical or chemical factor.

Energy Flow and Trophic Roles
==============================================

* Autotrophs (producers) make food using sunlight.
* Making food from sunlight is photosynthesis.
* Heterotrophs (consumers) eat other organisms for energy.
* Herbivores eat plants, carnivores eat meat.
* Omnivores eat both plants and meat.
* Scavengers, detritivores, and decomposers clean up.
* Decomposers break down dead matter and waste.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Autotroph**: A producer making food using sunlight.
  * **Heterotroph**: A consumer that eats other organisms.
  * **Herbivore**: An organism that eats only plants.
  * **Carnivore**: An organism that eats only meat.
  * **Omnivore**: An organism eating both plants and animals.
  * **Decomposer**: Breaks down dead material using external digestion.

    3. A goat is a plant-eating @@herbivore@@.

Ecosystem Interactions
=======================

* Organisms compete when vital resources are limited.
* Resources include food, water, light, space, mates.
* Competition harms both competing individuals.
* Competitors must use extra energy.
* Predators and prey keep populations balanced.
* Abiotic changes affect prey survival rates.
* Predator numbers also affect prey survival.


* **Key Terms:**

  * **Resource**: Anything an organism needs for survival.
  * **Competition**: Organisms fighting for the same limited resources.
  * **Predator**: An organism that hunts and eats others.
  * **Prey**: An organism hunted and eaten by predators.


.. cloze::

    1. Limited resources increase @@competition@@ between organisms.
    2. Competition affects an organism's ability to @@survive@@.
    3. A @@predator@@ hunts and eats its @@prey@@.
    4. More prey helps predators @@reproduce@@ and @@grow@@.
