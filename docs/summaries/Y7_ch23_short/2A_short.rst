2A Introduction to Classification
===================================

What is Classification?
===================================================

* Classification sorts organisms into groups.
* Groups are based on shared features.
* Taxonomy is studying, grouping and naming organisms.
* Scientists who classify organisms are taxonomists.
* New species are checked for unique traits.
* New species join existing or new groups.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Classification**: The process of grouping things together.
  * **Taxonomy**: The science of classifying and naming organisms.
  * **Taxonomist**: A scientist who organizes living things.
  * **Organism**: An individual living thing or life form.
  * **Species**: A group of closely related organisms.

Steps and Importance of Classification
===================================================

* Scientists first find similarities and differences.
* Next, scientists look at potential categories.
* Finally, scientists choose the best group.
* Grouping helps us understand survival needs.
* Grouping shows how closely organisms are related.
* Classification helps identify endangered species.
* Scientists can then protect endangered species.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Similarities**: Characteristics or traits that are the same.
  * **Endangered**: At risk of dying out completely.
  * **Extinction**: The complete dying out of a species.
  * **Conservation**: Protecting organisms and natural resources for the future.
