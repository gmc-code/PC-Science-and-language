How Classification Changed Over Time
====================================================================

* Early scientists grouped living things based only on what they could see with their eyes.
* The invention of the microscope allowed scientists to see tiny organisms and micro-structures for the first time.
* Aristotle was one of the first people to group organisms using systematic observation.
* Carl Linnaeus later created a formal system for naming and ordering species using binomial nomenclature.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Taxonomy**: The scientific study of how living things are classified.
  * **Microscope**: A tool used to make small objects look larger.
  * **Binomial Nomenclature**: A two-word naming system for organisms.

.. cloze::

    1. Early scientists grouped organisms using @@visible@@ traits, but the @@microscope@@ revealed hidden cellular structures.
    2. Aristotle used direct @@observation@@ to classify nature, while Carl Linnaeus developed modern @@binomial@@ nomenclature.

The Five Kingdoms of Life
==================================

* Scientists expanded the classification system into five distinct kingdoms as technology improved.
* Kingdom Animalia includes multicellular organisms that eat other living things for energy.
* Kingdom Plantae consists of green plants that make their own food through photosynthesis.
* Fungi absorb nutrients from decaying organic matter, while Monera includes tiny single-celled organisms like bacteria.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Autotroph**: An organism that makes its own food.
  * **Heterotroph**: An organism that consumes other living things for energy.
  * **Prokaryote**: A microscopic single-celled organism without a nucleus.

.. cloze::

    1. Organisms in Kingdom Plantae are @@autotrophic@@, whereas members of Kingdom Animalia are @@heterotrophic@@.
    2. Bacteria belong to Kingdom @@Monera@@ and lack a membrane-bound @@nucleus@@.

Modern Domains and Grammar Skills
==================================

* Scientists added three higher categories called domains after studying cell genetics.
* Nominalisation turns everyday action verbs into scientific nouns to make writing clearer.
* Transforming words like "classify" into "classification" builds formal scientific language.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Domain**: The highest rank of biological classification above kingdom.
  * **Nominalisation**: The process of changing verbs or adjectives into nouns.

.. cloze::

    1. The highest taxonomic rank is the @@domain@@, which divides life based on @@genetic@@ evidence.
    2. Changing process verbs into scientific nouns is called @@nominalisation@@, which improves text @@cohesion@@.
