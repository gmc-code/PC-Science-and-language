2A Introduction to Classification
===================================

What is Classification?
===================================================

* Classification is the process of sorting living things or organisms into groups based on their shared features and differences.
* The science of studying, grouping, and naming organisms is called taxonomy, which is performed by scientists known as taxonomists.
* When scientists discover a new species, they look at its unique traits to see if it fits into an existing group or needs a new group.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Classification**: The process of grouping things together.
  * **Taxonomy**: The science of classifying and naming organisms.
  * **Taxonomist**: A scientist who organizes living things into categories.
  * **Organism**: An individual living thing.
  * **Species**: A group of closely related organisms.

Steps and Importance of Classification
===================================================

* To classify an organism, scientists first find its similarities and differences, look at potential categories, and then choose the best group.
* Grouping species helps scientists understand their survival needs and see how closely related different living things are to each other.
* Classification allows scientists to identify endangered species and take action to protect them from complete extinction.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Similarities**: Characteristics or traits that are the same.
  * **Endangered**: At risk of dying out or becoming extinct.
  * **Extinction**: The complete dying out of a species.
  * **Conservation**: Protecting organisms and natural resources for the future.
