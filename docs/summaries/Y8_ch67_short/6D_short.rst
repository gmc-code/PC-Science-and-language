============================================
6D: Everyday Energy Efficiency
============================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy Rating Labels show how efficient an appliance is.
* More stars means a more efficient appliance.
* Labels help compare similar appliance models.
* Yearly electricity use is measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
* Power is the rate a device transforms energy.
* Power is measured in watts; one watt is one joule per second.
* Running cost = energy used (kWh) × price per kWh.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Energy Rating Label**: A star label comparing appliance efficiency.
  * **Kilowatt-hour (kWh)**: The unit for measuring household electricity use.
  * **Power**: The rate a device transforms energy.
  * **Watt (W)**: The unit of power; one joule per second.
  * **Running Cost**: Money spent operating an appliance over time.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Modern appliances use efficient technology, like LED lighting.
* Better insulation reduces heat flow into or out of appliances.
* Passive solar design uses sunlight and airflow to warm and cool homes.
* North-facing windows and wide eaves are passive solar features.
* Thermal mass materials include concrete and mudbrick.
* Thermal mass absorbs heat and releases it slowly.
* Energy audits help identify wasted energy.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Insulation**: Material that traps air to reduce heat flow.
  * **Passive Solar Design**: Using sunlight and airflow to heat and cool homes.
  * **Thermal Mass**: Materials that absorb heat and release it slowly.
  * **Energy Audit**: An inspection checking home energy use.
