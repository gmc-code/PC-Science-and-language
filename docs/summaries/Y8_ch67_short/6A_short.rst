================================================
6A Forms of Energy
================================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy is the ability to make changes.
* Energy can also move objects or do work.
* Potential energy is stored energy.
* Potential energy depends on an object's position or state.
* Kinetic energy is the energy of a moving object.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Energy**: The ability to make changes or do work.
  * **Potential Energy**: Stored energy due to position or state.
  * **Kinetic Energy**: The energy of a moving object.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Thermal energy is the energy of moving particles.
* Particles in hotter objects move faster.
* Chemical energy is stored in chemical bonds.
* Food, fuel and batteries store chemical energy.
* Raised objects store gravitational potential energy.
* Stretched or squeezed materials store elastic energy.
* Springs and rubber bands store elastic energy.
* Sound energy is carried by vibrating particles, such as air.
* Sound travels through the air to our ears.
* Electrical energy is carried by moving charged particles.
* Radiant energy travels as electromagnetic waves, such as light.
* Light energy can travel through empty space.
* Nuclear energy is released when atomic nuclei split or combine.
* Mechanical energy is the energy of moving parts in machines.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Thermal Energy**: The energy of moving particles in a substance.
  * **Chemical Energy**: Energy stored in chemical bonds.
  * **Gravitational Energy**: Stored energy of objects raised above the ground.
  * **Elastic Energy**: Energy stored in stretched or squeezed materials.
  * **Radiant Energy**: Energy carried by electromagnetic waves.
  * **Nuclear Energy**: Energy released from atomic nuclei.
