====================================
7B: Series and Parallel Circuits
====================================

Summary of Key Concepts
----------------------------------------

* Electricity needs a closed circuit to flow.
* The path must be unbroken.
* Conductors let electric current flow easily.
* Simple circuits contain a power supply and load.
* Connecting wires join these parts together.
* Batteries provide voltage and current for circuits.
* Voltage is the electrical push moving energy.
* Voltmeters measure voltage in volts.
* Current is the flow of electric charge.
* Ammeters measure current in amps.
* Resistance opposes or slows electric current.
* Opening a switch breaks the circuit loop.
* All electrical current then stops.
