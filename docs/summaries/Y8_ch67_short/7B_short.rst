====================================
7B: Series and Parallel Circuits
====================================

Summary of Key Concepts
----------------------------------------

* Current only flows in a closed, unbroken circuit.
* Conductors let electric current flow easily.
* Simple circuits contain a power supply, a load and a switch.
* Connecting wires join these parts together.
* Batteries provide the voltage that pushes current around a circuit.
* Voltage is the electrical push that moves energy.
* Voltage is measured in volts using a voltmeter.
* Voltmeters are connected in parallel.
* Current is the flow of electric charge.
* Current is measured in amps using an ammeter.
* Ammeters are connected in series.
* Resistance reduces the current in a circuit.
* Opening a switch breaks the loop and stops the current.
* A multimeter can measure voltage, current and resistance.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Conductor**: A material that lets current flow easily.
  * **Load**: A component that transforms electrical energy into other forms.
  * **Voltage**: The electrical push, measured in volts.
  * **Current**: The flow of electric charge, measured in amps.
  * **Resistance**: How much a component reduces current.
