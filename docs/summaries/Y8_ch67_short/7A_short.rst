====================================
7A: Electric Circuits
====================================

Summary of Key Concepts
----------------------------------------

* A series circuit has one single pathway.
* In series, current flows through every component in turn.
* A parallel circuit splits into several branches.
* In parallel, each component has its own branch.
* Opening a switch in a series circuit turns off every bulb.
* In parallel, if one branch is switched off, the others keep working.
* Current is the same throughout a series circuit.
* Series components share the total voltage.
* Each parallel branch gets the full battery voltage.
* Houses use parallel circuits so appliances work independently.
* Circuit breakers cut power during short circuits or overloads.
* Circuit breakers prevent overheating and fires.
* Safety switches (RCDs) protect people from electric shock.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Series Circuit**: A circuit with one continuous pathway.
  * **Parallel Circuit**: A circuit that splits into separate branches.
  * **Circuit Breaker**: A device that cuts power to prevent fires.
  * **Safety Switch (RCD)**: A device that cuts power to prevent electric shock.
