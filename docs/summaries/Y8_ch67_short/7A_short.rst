====================================
7A: Electric Circuits
====================================

Summary of Key Concepts
----------------------------------------

* A series circuit has one single pathway.
* Current flows through every component in turn.
* A parallel circuit branches into multiple pathways.
* Each component has its own separate loop.
* Switching off a series circuit stops all bulbs.
* In parallel, other branches keep working.
* Current is the same throughout a series circuit.
* Series components share the total voltage.
* Each parallel branch gets the full battery voltage.
* Houses use parallel circuits for independent appliances.
* Circuit breakers cut power during short circuits.
* Circuit breakers prevent fires and electric shock.
