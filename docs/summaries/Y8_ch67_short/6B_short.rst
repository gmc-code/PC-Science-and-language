================================================
6B Energy Transfers & Transformations
================================================

Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy cannot be created or destroyed.
* This is the law of conservation of energy.
* Energy can only be transferred or transformed.
* An energy transfer moves energy between objects in the same form.
* An energy transformation changes energy into another form.
* Transformations often happen inside a device.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Conservation of Energy**: Energy cannot be created or destroyed.
  * **Energy Transfer**: Energy moving between objects in the same form.
  * **Energy Transformation**: Energy changing from one form to another.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Flow diagrams use arrows to show energy changes.
* Hairdryers transform electrical energy into thermal energy.
* Hairdryers also produce kinetic and sound energy.
* Phone batteries transform chemical energy into electrical energy.
* Sankey diagrams show useful and wasted energy.
* The width of each Sankey arrow shows the amount of energy.
* Total input energy always equals total output energy.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Flow Diagram**: A diagram using arrows to show energy changes.
  * **Sankey Diagram**: A diagram whose arrow widths show amounts of energy.
  * **Input Energy**: The starting energy put into a system.
  * **Output Energy**: The energy coming out of a process.
  * **Waste Energy**: Output energy that is not useful.
