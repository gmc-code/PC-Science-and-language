Summary of Key Concepts 1
----------------------------------------

* Energy by-products are extra, usually unwanted, forms of energy.
* By-products form during energy transfers or transformations.
* Input energy is the energy going into a device.
* Output energy is the energy coming out of a device.
* Useful energy is output energy we need.
* Waste energy is output energy we don't use.
* Waste energy is usually thermal energy, sound or light.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **By-product**: An extra, usually unwanted, form of energy.
  * **Useful Energy**: Output energy that is useful to us.
  * **Waste Energy**: Output energy that is not useful.

Summary of Key Concepts 2
----------------------------------------

* Efficient devices turn most input energy into useful energy.
* Efficient devices waste very little energy.
* Conduction transfers heat through direct particle contact.
* Conduction occurs in solids, liquids and gases, but works best in solids.
* Convection transfers heat through moving liquids or gases.
* Radiation transfers heat through electromagnetic waves.
* Radiation needs no particles, so it can cross empty space.

* **Key Terms:**

  * **Efficient**: Turning most input energy into useful output.
  * **Conduction**: Heat transfer through direct particle contact.
  * **Convection**: Heat transfer through moving liquids or gases.
  * **Radiation**: Heat transfer through electromagnetic waves.
