The Marble Ramp Investigation
-------------------------------

.. admonition:: About this practice task
    :class: note

    The SIS assessment is a **science inquiry skills** task based on one practical investigation about energy.
    This practice uses a similar practical: rolling a **marble** down a ramp from different heights.

    Questions cover:

    * **questioning and predicting** (variables, aim, hypothesis)
    * **planning and conducting** (method, safety, ethics)
    * **recording and processing** (tables, averages, graphs)
    * **analysing and evaluating** (trends, accuracy, precision, errors, improvements, conclusion)

    Learn the skills and sentence starters here. They apply to any investigation, not just this one.

How to use these pages
-------------------------------

| Each page covers a few sections. After each section:
| 1. **Fill in the gaps**: drag each word from the word bank into the correct gap (each word is used once).
| 2. **Check your understanding**: answer 5 multiple-choice questions drawn at random from a bank of 10. Reload the page for a new set.

| How to read the boxes:
| **Key ideas** — the science and skills you need to know.
| **In the marble investigation** — how the idea applies to this practical.
| **Watch out** — common ways to lose marks.
| **Sentence starter** — a frame to help you write a full-mark answer.
