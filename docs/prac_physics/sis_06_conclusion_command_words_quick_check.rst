15–17. Conclusion, Command Words and Quick Check
================================================

15. Writing a conclusion
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Key ideas
    :class: note

    A full conclusion includes:

    #. **The purpose:** restate the aim.
    #. **The hypothesis:** say whether it was **supported** or **not supported**.
    #. **The findings with data:** summarise the trend using numbers from your results.
    #. **The explanation:** use science (GPE → KE) to explain why it happened.

.. admonition:: Model conclusion
    :class: hint

    The purpose of this investigation was to find out how ramp height affects the distance a marble rolls along the floor.
    The results **supported** the hypothesis: as the ramp height increased from 5 cm to 20 cm, the average distance rolled increased from 29.7 cm to 117.0 cm.
    This is because a higher ramp gives the marble more gravitational potential energy at the start, which is transformed into more kinetic energy by the bottom of the ramp, so it rolls further before friction stops it.

.. admonition:: Watch out
    :class: warning

    Never say the hypothesis was "proven" or "right". Say it was **supported** (or not supported) by the results.

        Which words should be used about the hypothesis in a conclusion?

        [ ] Proven or disproven | Incorrect. Science does not prove hypotheses.
        [ ] Right or wrong | Incorrect. Use "supported" or "not supported".
        [ ] Accurate or inaccurate | Incorrect. These describe data, not a hypothesis.

    .. multichoice::
        :torf:

        [ ] True | Incorrect. Never say "proven" or "right".
        [x] False | Correct. Say the hypothesis was supported by the results.

    .. multichoice::

        What must the findings part of a conclusion include?

        [x] Numbers from the results | Correct. For example, 29.7 cm to 117.0 cm.
        [ ] Only words like "a lot" or "more" | Incorrect. You must use data.
        [ ] The method steps | Incorrect. Those belong in the method.
        [ ] The list of hazards | Incorrect. Those belong in the risk assessment.

    .. multichoice::

        Which science idea explains the marble results?

        [x] A higher ramp gives more GPE, which is transformed into more KE, so the marble rolls further before friction stops it. | Correct.
        [ ] A higher ramp makes the marble heavier. | Incorrect. Mass does not change.
        [ ] Friction increases with ramp height. | Incorrect. The ramp and floor surfaces are the same.
        [ ] Energy is created as the marble rolls. | Incorrect. Energy is transformed, not created.

    .. multichoice::

        Which sentence best states the findings with data?

        [x] As the ramp height increased from 5 cm to 20 cm, the average distance rolled increased from 29.7 cm to 117.0 cm. | Correct.
        [ ] The marble went much further. | Incorrect. No data is given.
        [ ] The experiment worked. | Incorrect. This does not state the findings.
        [ ] The ramp was higher. | Incorrect. This does not describe the DV.

    .. multichoice::

        If the results had shown that the distance decreased as the ramp height increased, the hypothesis would be...

        [x] not supported | Correct. The hypothesis predicted the distance would increase.
        [ ] supported | Incorrect. The results go against the prediction.
        [ ] proven wrong | Incorrect. Use "not supported", not "proven".
        [ ] still correct | Incorrect. The results do not match the prediction.

    .. multichoice::

        In the model conclusion, which part is the explanation?

        [x] A higher ramp gives the marble more GPE, which is transformed into more KE by the bottom of the ramp. | Correct. It uses science to explain why.
        [ ] The purpose of this investigation was to find out how ramp height affects distance rolled. | Incorrect. That is the purpose.
        [ ] The results supported the hypothesis. | Incorrect. That is the hypothesis statement.
        [ ] The distance increased from 29.7 cm to 117.0 cm. | Incorrect. That is the findings with data.

    .. multichoice::

        Which part of a conclusion uses the word "because"?

        [x] The explanation | Correct. It explains why the results happened.
        [ ] The purpose | Incorrect. The purpose restates the aim.
        [ ] The data | Incorrect. The data gives the numbers.
        [ ] None of it | Incorrect. The explanation should give a reason.

16. Command words: what the question wants
------------------------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :widths: 30 70
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Command word
     - What to do
   * - Identify
     - Name it. A word or short phrase is enough.
   * - Describe
     - Say **what** happens, with details or data.
   * - Explain
     - Say **what** happens AND **why** (use "because").
   * - Predict
     - Say what you think **will** happen, and give a reason.
   * - Calculate
     - Show the working and give the answer **with units**.
   * - Compare
     - Give similarities **and** differences, using words like "whereas" or "higher than".
   * - Justify / with reference to the data
     - Back up your answer with **numbers** from the table or graph.

        What does the command word "Identify" ask you to do?

    .. multichoice::

        What does "Describe" ask you to do?

        [x] Say what happens, with details or data | Correct.
        [ ] Say what happens and why | Incorrect. "Explain" also needs the reason.
        [ ] Name it in one word | Incorrect. That is "Identify".
        [ ] Say what will happen | Incorrect. That is "Predict".

    .. multichoice::

        What does "Explain" ask you to do?

        [x] Say what happens AND why, using "because" | Correct.
        [ ] Only say what happens | Incorrect. That is "Describe".
        [ ] Only name it | Incorrect. That is "Identify".
        [ ] Only give numbers | Incorrect. "Explain" needs a reason.

    .. multichoice::

        What does "Predict" ask you to do?

        [x] Say what you think will happen, and give a reason | Correct.
        [ ] Say what has already happened | Incorrect. That is "Describe".
        [ ] Show the working | Incorrect. That is "Calculate".
        [ ] Name a variable | Incorrect. That is "Identify".

    .. multichoice::

        What does "Calculate" ask you to do?

        [x] Show the working and give the answer with units | Correct.
        [ ] Give an estimate without working | Incorrect. Working must be shown.
        [ ] Give the answer without units | Incorrect. Units are needed.
        [ ] Describe the trend | Incorrect. That is "Describe".

    .. multichoice::

        What does "Compare" ask you to do?

        [x] Give similarities and differences | Correct. Use words like "whereas" or "higher than".
        [ ] Describe only one thing | Incorrect. Compare needs at least two things.
        [ ] Give only similarities | Incorrect. Differences are needed too.
        [ ] Name it | Incorrect. That is "Identify".

    .. multichoice::

        What does "Justify" or "with reference to the data" ask you to do?

        [x] Back up your answer with numbers from the table or graph | Correct.
        [ ] Give your opinion only | Incorrect. You need evidence from the data.
        [ ] Draw a new graph | Incorrect. Use the data you have.
        [ ] Repeat the question | Incorrect. You must give evidence.

    .. multichoice::

        A question asks: "Explain why the marble stopped rolling." Which answer is best?

        [x] The marble stopped because friction and air resistance transformed its kinetic energy into heat and sound. | Correct. It says what happened and why.
        [ ] The marble stopped. | Incorrect. There is no reason.
        [ ] Friction. | Incorrect. This only identifies; it does not explain.
        [ ] The marble rolled 117.0 cm. | Incorrect. This is data, not an explanation.

    .. multichoice::

        A question asks: "Compare the precision of the results at 15 cm and 20 cm." Which answer is best?

        [x] The trials at 15 cm had a range of 3 cm, whereas the trials at 20 cm had a range of 15 cm, so the 15 cm results were more precise. | Correct. It compares both, using data.
        [ ] The 15 cm results were good. | Incorrect. It only mentions one height and gives no data.
        [ ] The 20 cm results had an outlier. | Incorrect. It does not compare the two heights.
        [ ] Both heights were tested three times. | Incorrect. This does not compare precision.

    .. multichoice::

        A question asks: "Identify the dependent variable." Which answer is enough?

        [x] The distance the marble rolls (cm) | Correct. A short phrase is enough.
        [ ] A long paragraph about energy | Incorrect. "Identify" only needs a name.
        [ ] The ramp height (cm) | Incorrect. That is the independent variable.
        [ ] Because it depends on the IV | Incorrect. This does not name the variable.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the independent variable in the marble investigation?

        [x] The height of the ramp (cm) | Correct.
        [ ] The distance the marble rolls (cm) | Incorrect. That is the dependent variable.
        [ ] The type of marble | Incorrect. That is a controlled variable.
        [ ] The floor surface | Incorrect. That is a controlled variable.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the dependent variable?

        [x] The distance the marble rolls along the floor from the end of the ramp (cm) | Correct.
        [ ] The height of the ramp (cm) | Incorrect. That is the independent variable.
        [ ] The release point | Incorrect. That is a controlled variable.
        [ ] The number of trials | Incorrect. This is part of the method.

    .. multichoice::

        Which TWO of these are controlled variables?

        [x] Same marble | Correct.
        [x] Same floor surface | Correct.
        [ ] Ramp height | Incorrect. That is the independent variable.
        [ ] Distance rolled | Incorrect. That is the dependent variable.

    .. multichoice::

        What energy transformation happens as the marble rolls down the ramp?

        [x] Gravitational potential energy → kinetic energy | Correct.
        [ ] Kinetic energy → gravitational potential energy | Incorrect. That is the wrong way around.
        [ ] Heat → kinetic energy | Incorrect. Heat is produced as the marble slows.
        [ ] Sound → gravitational potential energy | Incorrect. These are not the forms involved.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the average of 58 cm, 64 cm and 61 cm?

        [x] 61.0 cm | Correct. (58 + 64 + 61) ÷ 3 = 183 ÷ 3 = 61.0 cm.
        [ ] 183.0 cm | Incorrect. That is the total; divide by 3.
        [ ] 6.0 cm | Incorrect. That is the range.
        [ ] 64.0 cm | Incorrect. That is the largest trial.

    .. multichoice::

        Which axis does the independent variable go on?

        [x] The x-axis (horizontal) | Correct.
        [ ] The y-axis (vertical) | Incorrect. The DV goes on the y-axis.
        [ ] Either axis | Incorrect. The IV always goes on the x-axis.
        [ ] Neither axis | Incorrect. The IV must be graphed.

    .. multichoice::

        What is the difference between accuracy and precision?

        [x] Accuracy is how close to the true value; precision is how close the repeated results are to each other. | Correct.
        [ ] Accuracy is how close the repeated results are; precision is how close to the true value. | Incorrect. These are swapped.
        [ ] They mean the same thing. | Incorrect. They compare different things.
        [ ] Accuracy is about safety; precision is about fairness. | Incorrect. Both are about data quality.

    .. multichoice::

        Trials of 118, 124 and 109 cm have what range? Is this high or low precision?

        [x] 15 cm, which is lower precision than the other heights | Correct. 124 − 109 = 15 cm.
        [ ] 15 cm, which is high precision | Incorrect. A large range means low precision.
        [ ] 6 cm, which is high precision | Incorrect. Use the largest and smallest: 124 − 109.
        [ ] 117 cm, which is high precision | Incorrect. 117 cm is the average.

    .. multichoice::

        What error happens when you read a ruler from an angle?

        [x] Parallax error | Correct.
        [ ] Random error | Incorrect. Random errors cause unpredictable scatter.
        [ ] Systematic error | Incorrect. Parallax is its own named reading error.
        [ ] Bias | Incorrect. Bias is about changing or ignoring results.

    .. multichoice::

        How do repeated trials improve results?

        [x] Averaging more trials reduces the effect of random errors and makes the results more reliable. | Correct.
        [ ] They remove systematic errors. | Incorrect. Averaging does not fix systematic errors.
        [ ] They make the marble roll further. | Incorrect. Trials do not change the distance.
        [ ] They prove the hypothesis. | Incorrect. Hypotheses are never proven.

    .. multichoice::

        A student changes a result to match the true value. Why is this wrong?

        [x] It is dishonest (biased) and hides real errors; they should record it and repeat the trial. | Correct.
        [ ] It makes the average too small. | Incorrect. The problem is honesty.
        [ ] It is fine because the true value is correct. | Incorrect. Changing data is always dishonest.
        [ ] It changes the independent variable. | Incorrect. It changes a recorded result.

    .. multichoice::

        If the ramp is covered in sandpaper, will the marble roll further or less far? Why?

        [x] Less far, because more friction transforms more of its energy into heat and sound, leaving less kinetic energy. | Correct.
        [ ] Further, because sandpaper gives more grip. | Incorrect. More friction wastes more energy.
        [ ] The same distance, because the height is the same. | Incorrect. Extra friction reduces the KE.
        [ ] Further, because it gains more GPE. | Incorrect. GPE depends on height, not the surface.
