================================================
3–5. Variables, Aim and Hypothesis
================================================

3. Variables
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Key ideas
    :class: note

    * The **independent variable (IV)** is the one thing you **change** on purpose. (I change it.)
    * The **dependent variable (DV)** is what you **measure**. It depends on the IV.
    * **Controlled variables** are everything else that you keep **the same**, so that only the IV affects the DV. This makes it a **fair test** (a valid experiment).
    * Tip: the research question usually contains both: "How does the **IV** affect the **DV**?"

.. list-table::
   :widths: 30 70
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Variable
     - In the marble investigation
   * - Independent (IV)
     - The height of the ramp (cm)
   * - Dependent (DV)
     - The distance the marble rolls along the floor from the end of the ramp (cm)
   * - Controlled
     - Same marble • same release point on the ramp • marble released, not pushed • same ramp (length and surface) • same floor surface • distance measured from the end of the ramp each time

.. admonition:: Explaining why a variable must be controlled
    :class: hint

    * **Same marble:** a bigger or heavier marble may roll a different distance, so we could not tell whether the ramp height caused the change.
    * **Same floor surface:** carpet has more friction than tiles and would stop the marble sooner, changing the distance rolled.
    * **Released, not pushed:** a push gives the marble extra kinetic energy, so it would roll further for reasons other than the ramp height.

.. admonition:: Watch out
    :class: warning

    Don't just name the variable – an "explain why" question needs **how it would affect the results** (the DV).
    Include **units** where they apply, e.g. ramp height (cm).

4. Writing an aim
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Key ideas
    :class: note

    * An **aim** states what the experiment is trying to find out.
    * It is written as a statement, not a question.
    * A good aim names **both the IV and the DV**.

.. admonition:: Sentence starter
    :class: tip

    *To investigate the effect of [independent variable] on [dependent variable].*

.. admonition:: In the marble investigation
    :class: hint

    To investigate the effect of ramp height on the distance a marble rolls along the floor.

5. Writing a hypothesis
------------------------------------------------

.. admonition:: Key ideas
    :class: note

    * A **hypothesis** is a testable **prediction** about the relationship between the IV and the DV.
    * A full-mark hypothesis has two parts: **(1) the prediction** (what will happen to the DV when the IV changes) and **(2) the scientific reason** (why, using science ideas).
    * Use direction words: increase, decrease, or stay the same.

.. admonition:: Sentence starter
    :class: tip

    *If the [IV] increases, then the [DV] will [increase / decrease] because [scientific reason].*

.. admonition:: In the marble investigation
    :class: hint

    If the ramp is made higher, then the marble will roll a greater distance along the floor, because it starts with more gravitational potential energy, which is transformed into more kinetic energy by the bottom of the ramp.

.. admonition:: Watch out
    :class: warning

    "The marble will go further" is only half a hypothesis – it must mention the IV and give a **because** reason.
