Electricity Embedded Clauses
====================================================

Embedded Clauses as Qualifiers in Noun Groups
--------------------------------------------------------------

| Qualifers are words or phrases that provide additional information about a noun. In this section, we will focus on embedded clauses that serve as Qualifiers in noun groups. An embedded clause is a dependent clause that is inserted within a noun group to give more detail about the Thing being described.
| Embedded clauses can be introduced by relative pronouns such as "that," "which," or "who," and they provide essential or non-essential information about the noun.

Noun Group = :premodifier:`any premodifiers` + :thing:`Thing` + :embedded:`Qualifier`



.. textselect::
    :mode: thing
    :instructions: Highlight the thing in red after the main verb in green.

    Electrical circuits :processp:`provide` {{premodifier:a}} {{thing:pathway}} {{embedded:that allows electrons to flow}}.


.. textselect::
    :mode: thing
    :instructions: Highlight the thing in red after the main verb in green. The premodifier is marked in orange.

    A battery :processp:`is` :premodifier:`a power` {{thing:supply}} {{embedded:that provides electrical energy to the circuit}}.


.. textselect::
    :mode: embedded
    :instructions: Highlight the embedded clause that qualifies the noun that is marked in red.

    Voltage :processp:`represents` :premodifier:`the amount of electric potential` :thing:`energy` {{embedded:that charge carries}}.

.. textselect::
    :mode: multi
    :instructions: Highlight the premodifier, thing, and embedded clause that follow the main verb marked in green.

    A multimeter :processp:`is` {{premodifier:a versatile}} {{thing:tool}} {{embedded:that can measure resistance in ohms}}.
