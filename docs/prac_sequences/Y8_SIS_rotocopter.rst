=================================
Y8 SIS rotocopter prac sequence
=================================

Use the full screen icon first. **Drag** a picture into a box, or **tap** a picture and then
tap a box.


.. imagesequence:: Y8 SIS rotocopter method
   :answer-key:
   :captions: slot
   :fixed: 1, 7
   :feedback: count
   :arrows: right
   :columns: 3
   :fullscreen-columns: 4
   :tray-columns: 4
   :fullscreen-tray-columns: 2
   :fullscreen-tray-width: 50
   :print: cards
   :theme: sunset

   .. step:: images/Y8_SIS_rotocopter/rotocopter_step1.png

      Place a paperclip at the base to complete the rotocopter

   .. step:: images/Y8_SIS_rotocopter/rotocopter_step2.png

      In your results table, note the first length on the wings

   .. step:: images/Y8_SIS_rotocopter/rotocopter_step3.png

      Drop the rotocopter from the same height three times, timing how long it takes to fall in each trial

   .. step:: images/Y8_SIS_rotocopter/rotocopter_step4.png

      Now using a pair of scissors cut the blue solid lines on the wings

   .. step:: images/Y8_SIS_rotocopter/rotocopter_step5.png

      Record the new length of the wings in your table

   .. step:: images/Y8_SIS_rotocopter/rotocopter_step6.png

      Repeat steps 1-4 for the new blade length

   .. step:: images/Y8_SIS_rotocopter/rotocopter_step7.png

      Repeat steps 1-4 after cutting the orange lines and recording the final blade length
