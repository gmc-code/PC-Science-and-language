========================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Role)
========================================

| Role qualifiers are used to describe the role or function of something.
| Role qualifiers answer one of two core probe questions depending on their grammatical sub-function:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Guise**
     - *What as?*
     - *as, by way of, in the capacity of, in the form of, in the role of*
   * - **Product**
     - *What into?*
     - *into, to*

----

Guise (*What as?*)
------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **as**
     - :qualifier:`as a control`
     - Job or function something performs
   * - **by way of**
     - :qualifier:`by way of demonstration`
     - Something serving as an illustration
   * - **in the capacity of**
     - :qualifier:`in the capacity of lab monitor`
     - Formal statement of a position held
   * - **in the form of**
     - :qualifier:`in the form of heat`
     - The shape or state something takes
   * - **in the role of**
     - :qualifier:`in the role of decomposers`
     - Part played within a larger system

Role Qualifiers: Guise
----------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    Hydrogen {{as a fuel}} produces water when it burns.
    The bacteria {{in the role of decomposers}} keep a compost heap warm.
    The energy {{in the form of heat}} spreads through the metal spoon.
    Carbon dioxide {{as a greenhouse gas}} traps heat in the atmosphere.
    The mineral {{in the form of crystals}} grew slowly in the dish.

----

Product (*What into?*)
----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **into**
     - :qualifier:`into glucose`
     - Substance or form created by a change
   * -
     - :qualifier:`into chemical energy`
     - Energy transformed from one type to another
   * - **to**
     - :qualifier:`to steam`
     - Result of a change of state
   * -
     - :qualifier:`to a solid`
     - New state reached after a change

Role Qualifiers: Product
------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The conversion {{into glucose}} happens inside the chloroplasts.
    The change {{to steam}} makes a kettle whistle.
    The transformation {{into chemical energy}} stores the Sun's energy in the leaf.
    The change {{to a solid}} happens when the wax cools.
    The breakdown of glucose {{into carbon dioxide and water}} releases energy for the cell.

Role Qualifiers: Choose the Qualifier
-------------------------------------

.. cloze::
    :instructions: Complete the following sentences by dragging the correct qualifiers into the blanks. Use the probe question in brackets to help you decide.

    Fungi @@in the role of decomposers@@ return nutrients to the soil. (What as, what part is played within a larger system?)
    The energy @@in the form of light@@ from the Sun reaches Earth through space. (What as, what shape or state does the energy take?)
    The tray of seedlings @@as a control@@ received plain water. (What as, what job or function does it perform?)
    The metamorphosis @@into a butterfly@@ happens inside the chrysalis. (What into, what form results from the change?)
    The change @@to a liquid@@ happens when steam touches a cold window. (What into, what new state is reached?)
