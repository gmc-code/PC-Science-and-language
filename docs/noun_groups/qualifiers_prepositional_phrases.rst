========================================
Prepositional Phrases as Qualifiers
========================================

| A **prepositional phrase** is a structural group (preposition + noun group) that functions as a **Qualifier** when placed directly after a head noun.
| When they modify a noun, prepositional phrases act like an **adjective** and so are also known as **adjectival prepositional phrases**.
| Prepositional phrases can be used to specify the location, material, purpose, possession, condition, or features of a noun.


.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 60

   * - Category
     - Probe Question
     - Example
   * - 1. Time
     - *When / How long / How often?*
     - The energy increase :qualifier:`during the chemical reaction` was measured.
   * - 2. Place
     - *Where / Where to / Where from?*
     - The test tube :qualifier:`on the rack` contains acid.
   * - 3. Manner
     - *How / What like / What degree?*
     - A solid precipitate :qualifier:`like chalk` formed at the bottom.
   * - 4. Accompaniment
     - *With what / Without what?*
     - A chemical solution :qualifier:`with high acidity` reacts quickly.
   * - 5. Matter
     - *About what / In respect to what?*
     - A thin strip :qualifier:`of magnesium ribbon` was ignited.
   * - 6. Cause
     - *Why / For what purpose / On whose behalf?*
     - The safety valve :qualifier:`for the gas tap` must remain open.
   * - 7. Contingency
     - *Under what conditions / Despite what?*
     - Water :qualifier:`at boiling point` turns into gas.
   * - 8. Role
     - *What as / Into what?*
     - The copper strip :qualifier:`as a catalyst` accelerated the reaction.
   * - 9. Angle
     - *According to whom / From whose perspective?*
     - The experimental data :qualifier:`from the research team` confirmed the hypothesis.
