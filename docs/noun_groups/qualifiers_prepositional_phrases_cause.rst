=========================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Cause)
=========================================

| Cause qualifiers are used to explain the cause of something or the circumstances that led to it.
| Cause qualifiers answer one of three core probe questions depending on their grammatical sub-function:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Reason**
     - *Why?*
     - *as a result of, because of, due to, from, on account of, owing to, thanks to*
   * - **Purpose**
     - *For what purpose?*
     - *for*
   * - **Behalf**
     - *For whom? / On whose behalf?*
     - *for, for the benefit of, on behalf of*

----

Reason (*Why?*)
---------------

Reason qualifiers explain the cause of something or the circumstances that led to it.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **as a result of**
     - :qualifier:`as a result of the reaction`
     - Event that led to an outcome
   * - **because of**
     - :qualifier:`because of warm water`
     - Direct and general cause
   * - **due to**
     - :qualifier:`due to friction`
     - Cause of a measurable effect
   * - **from**
     - :qualifier:`from acid rain`
     - Source or cause of damage or change
   * - **on account of**
     - :qualifier:`on account of the storm`
     - Formal statement of the reason
   * - **owing to**
     - :qualifier:`owing to the drought`
     - Formal cause of an effect
   * - **thanks to**
     - :qualifier:`thanks to the extra sunshine`
     - Cause with a positive outcome


Cause Qualifiers: Reason
------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The coral bleaching {{because of warm water}} worried the divers.
    The temperature rise {{as a result of the reaction}} was recorded every minute.
    The energy loss {{due to friction}} was greater on the carpet.
    The delay {{on account of the storm}} lasted two hours.
    The damage {{from acid rain}} was clear on the old statue.
    The record harvest {{thanks to the extra sunshine}} surprised the farmers.
    The low river level {{owing to the drought}} stranded the fish.

----

Purpose (*For what purpose?*)
-----------------------------

Purpose qualifiers describe the intended function, use or aim of a noun.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **for**
     - :qualifier:`for safety`
     - Intended function or benefit
   * -
     - :qualifier:`for accuracy`
     - Intended improvement in precision
   * -
     - :qualifier:`for comparison`
     - Purpose in an investigation
   * -
     - :qualifier:`for wildlife protection`
     - Intended protective function
   * -
     - :qualifier:`for holding hot glass`
     - Intended use of an object


Cause Qualifiers: Purpose
-------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The goggles {{for safety}} hang beside the door.
    The extra trials {{for accuracy}} took another lesson.
    The fence {{for wildlife protection}} is two metres high.
    The tongs {{for holding hot glass}} are kept in the drawer.
    The extra tray {{for comparison}} received plain water.

----

Behalf (*For whom? / On whose behalf?*)
---------------------------------------

Behalf qualifiers identify the person or group that something is intended for, that gains from it, or that someone represents. *For* also appears in Purpose, so use the probe question to decide: a Purpose qualifier names an aim (*for safety*), and a Behalf qualifier names people (*for the class*).

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **for**
     - :qualifier:`for the class`
     - Person or group for whom something is intended
   * - **for the benefit of**
     - :qualifier:`for the benefit of local farmers`
     - Person or group who gains from something
   * - **on behalf of**
     - :qualifier:`on behalf of the research team`
     - Person or group represented by someone


Cause Qualifiers: Behalf
------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The volcano demonstration {{for the class}} took twenty minutes.
    The soil survey {{for the benefit of local farmers}} was published in May.
    The presentation {{on behalf of the research team}} lasted ten minutes.
    The lung display {{for the visitors}} was made from a plastic bottle and balloons.
    The Earth model {{for the Year 7 students}} was built from coloured clay.

Cause Qualifiers: Choose the Qualifier
--------------------------------------

.. cloze::
    :instructions: Complete the following sentences by dragging the correct qualifiers into the blanks. Use the probe question in brackets to help you decide.

    The fish deaths @@because of low oxygen@@ alarmed the rangers. (Why? What was the cause?)
    The high tides @@owing to the Moon's gravity@@ are listed in the tide table. (Why? What cause explains the tides?)
    The filter paper @@for removing solids@@ sat in the funnel. (For what purpose? What is the intended use?)
    The bird hide @@for the visiting scientists@@ was built beside the pond. (For whom? Which person or group is it intended for?)
    The letter @@on behalf of the school@@ was sent to the weather bureau. (On whose behalf? Which group is represented?)
