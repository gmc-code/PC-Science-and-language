==========================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Manner)
==========================================

| Manner qualifiers describe the manner in which something is done.
| Manner qualifiers answer one of four core probe questions depending on their grammatical sub-function:

.. note::

    A Manner qualifier describes how something is done, so it follows a noun
    that names an action or process (the *heating*, the *separation*, the
    *timing*), not a noun that names an object.


.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Style**
     - *How?*
     - *at, in, under, with*
   * - **Means**
     - *By what means?*
     - *by, by means of, through, via*
   * - **Instrument**
     - *With what?*
     - *with, without*
   * - **Comparison**
     - *Compared with what?*
     - *like, similar to, unlike*

----

Style (*How?*)
--------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **at**
     - :qualifier:`at a steady rate`
     - Speed or rate of an action
   * - **in**
     - :qualifier:`in a spiral`
     - Shape or pattern of movement
   * - **under**
     - :qualifier:`under high pressure`
     - Conditions imposed on an action
   * - **with**
     - :qualifier:`with great care`
     - Quality or attitude of an action


Manner Qualifiers: Style
------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The heating {{at a steady rate}} produced a straight-line graph.
    The water flow {{in a spiral}} was filmed from above.
    The reaction {{under high pressure}} needs a strong container.
    The excavation {{with great care}} took three days.

----

Means (*By what means?*)
------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **by**
     - :qualifier:`by filtration`
     - Process used to achieve a result
   * -
     - :qualifier:`by hand`
     - Physical method of doing something
   * - **by means of**
     - :qualifier:`by means of electrolysis`
     - Formal statement of the process used
   * - **through**
     - :qualifier:`through distillation`
     - Process that leads to an outcome
   * - **via**
     - :qualifier:`via osmosis`
     - Route or process taken to achieve a result


Manner Qualifiers: Means
------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The separation {{by filtration}} was finished before the bell.
    The sorting {{by hand}} was slow and tiring.
    The plating {{by means of electrolysis}} took twenty minutes.
    The desalination {{through distillation}} uses a lot of energy.
    The water uptake {{via osmosis}} was slow.

----

Instrument (*With what?*)
-------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **with**
     - :qualifier:`with a pipette`
     - Tool used to perform an action
   * -
     - :qualifier:`with a stopwatch`
     - Equipment used to measure
   * - **without**
     - :qualifier:`without a lid`
     - Tool or item that is absent
   * -
     - :qualifier:`without gloves`
     - Protective equipment that is absent


Manner Qualifiers: Instrument
-----------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The timing {{with a stopwatch}} was repeated three times.
    The transfer {{with a pipette}} was easy for the class.
    The stirring {{with a glass rod}} lasted two minutes.
    The observation {{with a microscope}} took a whole lesson.
    The evaporation {{without a lid}} took only two days.
    The handling {{without gloves}} was not allowed.

----

Comparison (*Compared with what?*)
----------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **like**
     - :qualifier:`like a swing`
     - Resemblance to another process
   * - **similar to**
     - :qualifier:`similar to the control`
     - Close resemblance to a reference
   * - **unlike**
     - :qualifier:`unlike the control`
     - Difference from a reference


Manner Qualifiers: Comparison
-----------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The pendulum motion {{like a swing}} repeated every two seconds.
    The cooling rate {{similar to the control}} was plotted on the grid.
    The growth rate {{unlike the control}} was measured in centimetres per day.
    The energy release {{like a small explosion}} surprised the class.

Manner Qualifiers: Choose the Qualifier
---------------------------------------

.. cloze::
    :instructions: Complete the following sentences by dragging the correct qualifiers into the blanks. Use the probe question in brackets to help you decide.

    The stretching @@under a heavy load@@ made the spring longer. (How, what conditions are imposed on the action?)
    The purification @@by crystallisation@@ took two days. (By what means, what process is used to achieve the result?)
    The temperature reading @@with a data logger@@ was stored automatically. (With what, what tool or equipment is used?)
    The eruption @@like a fountain@@ lasted six hours. (Compared with what, which process or pattern does it resemble?)
    The reaction @@unlike the first trial@@ released no gas. (Compared with what, which trial or process is it different from?)
