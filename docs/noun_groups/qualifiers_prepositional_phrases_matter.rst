==========================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Matter)
==========================================

| Matter qualifiers are used to refer to a topic or relation in a sentence.
| Matter qualifiers answer one of two core probe questions depending on their grammatical sub-function:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Topic**
     - *About what?*
     - *about, concerning, on, over*
   * - **Relation**
     - *In regard to what?*
     - *as to, in relation to, in terms of, regarding, with regard to, with respect to*


----

Topic (*About what?*)
---------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **about**
     - :qualifier:`about the water cycle`
     - General subject being discussed
   * - **concerning**
     - :qualifier:`concerning the safety rules`
     - Formal statement of the subject
   * - **on**
     - :qualifier:`on photosynthesis`
     - Specialised or academic subject
   * - **over**
     - :qualifier:`over the experimental results`
     - Subject of a disagreement or debate


Matter Qualifiers: Topic
------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The report {{about the water cycle}} has six pages and two diagrams.
    The poster {{on photosynthesis}} hangs beside the window.
    The safety talk {{concerning the Bunsen burner}} covered hair and sleeves.
    The debate {{over the experimental results}} lasted all lesson.
    The article {{about volcanoes}} was written by a geologist.
    The quiz {{on the states of matter}} was marked out of ten.


----

Relation (*In regard to what?*)
-------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **as to**
     - :qualifier:`as to the cause of the error`
     - Points to the issue being decided
   * - **in relation to**
     - :qualifier:`in relation to the control`
     - Connection between two things
   * - **in terms of**
     - :qualifier:`in terms of mass`
     - Aspect or measure being considered
   * - **regarding**
     - :qualifier:`regarding the results`
     - Formal statement of the topic
   * - **with regard to**
     - :qualifier:`with regard to the method`
     - Formal focus on one part of a topic
   * - **with respect to**
     - :qualifier:`with respect to temperature`
     - Focus on one variable or factor


Matter Qualifiers: Relation
---------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The comment {{regarding the results}} was written in red pen.
    The question {{with regard to the method}} came from the teacher.
    The difference {{in terms of speed}} was clear from the results table.
    The prediction {{with respect to temperature}} was written before the test.
    The note {{as to the cause of the error}} was added to the report.
