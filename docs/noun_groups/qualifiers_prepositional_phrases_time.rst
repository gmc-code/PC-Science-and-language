=========================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Time)
=========================================

| A **time qualifier** is a prepositional phrase that comes after a noun and tells us when something happens, how long it lasts, or how often it occurs.
| Time qualifiers have three sub-functions, each with its own probe question:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Point in Time**
     - *When?*
     - *about, after, ahead of, around, as of, at, before, between, by, during, following, in, in advance of, in time for, on, past, prior to, upon*
   * - **Duration**
     - *How long?*
     - *across, between, for, for the duration of, from...until, in the course of, into, over, since, through, throughout, until, up to, within*
   * - **Frequency**
     - *How often?*
     - *at, before, in, on, per*

----

Point in Time (*When?*)
-----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **about**
     - :qualifier:`about midday`
     - Approximate time of day
   * - **after**
     - :qualifier:`after the experiment`
     - Later than a completed event
   * - **ahead of**
     - :qualifier:`ahead of the practical`
     - Prior to a scheduled event
   * - **around**
     - :qualifier:`around noon`
     - Near a specific target time
   * - **as of**
     - :qualifier:`as of Monday`
     - Starting from a specific date/time
   * - **at**
     - :qualifier:`at noon`
     - Specific time of day
   * -
     - :qualifier:`at the start of spring`
     - Specific point in a season/year
   * - **before**
     - :qualifier:`before the practical`
     - Prior to an event
   * - **between**
     - :qualifier:`between 2 and 3 pm`
     - Within a specific time window
   * - **by**
     - :qualifier:`by noon`
     - Latest deadline
   * - **during**
     - :qualifier:`during the storm`
     - Concurrent with an event
   * - **following**
     - :qualifier:`following the trial`
     - Immediately after an event
   * - **in**
     - :qualifier:`in the afternoon`
     - General part of the day
   * - **in advance of**
     - :qualifier:`in advance of the practical`
     - Ahead of a scheduled activity
   * - **in time for**
     - :qualifier:`in time for the bell`
     - Before a strict cut-off
   * - **on**
     - :qualifier:`on Monday`
     - Specific calendar day
   * - **past**
     - :qualifier:`past midnight`
     - Later than a set milestone
   * - **prior to**
     - :qualifier:`prior to the practical`
     - Formal reference before an event
   * - **upon**
     - :qualifier:`upon arrival`
     - Immediately triggered by an event


Time Qualifiers: Point in Time
---------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The temperature reading {{at noon}} showed a rise.
    The practical {{on Monday}} tested the soil.
    The shadow {{in the afternoon}} was the longest.
    The mass {{after the experiment}} was lower because gas escaped.
    The safety briefing {{before the practical}} covered goggles.
    The rainfall {{during the storm}} was very heavy.

Duration (*How long?*)
----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **across**
     - :qualifier:`across the summer term`
     - Multi-week or seasonal period
   * - **between**
     - :qualifier:`between March and June`
     - Span connecting two fixed dates
   * - **for**
     - :qualifier:`for three hours`
     - Fixed block of time
   * - **for the duration of**
     - :qualifier:`for the duration of the experiment`
     - Entire length of an event
   * - **from ... until**
     - :qualifier:`from dusk until dawn`
     - Span defined by start and end points
   * - **in the course of**
     - :qualifier:`in the course of the experiment`
     - Throughout the progression of a process
   * - **into**
     - :qualifier:`into the evening`
     - Extending into a subsequent period
   * - **over**
     - :qualifier:`over the week`
     - Across a multi-day span
   * -
     - :qualifier:`over thousands of years`
     - Extended geological or historical scale
   * - **since**
     - :qualifier:`since Monday`
     - Elapsed time up to the present
   * - **through**
     - :qualifier:`through the night`
     - Continuous from start to end
   * - **throughout**
     - :qualifier:`throughout the reaction`
     - Across every phase of a process
   * - **until / till**
     - :qualifier:`until Friday`
     - Continuous up to a specified end point
   * - **up to**
     - :qualifier:`up to three days`
     - Maximum time limit
   * - **within**
     - :qualifier:`within five days`
     - Inside an allowable time limit



Time Qualifiers: Duration
---------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The power cut {{for three hours}} spoiled the samples.
    The gentle stirring {{throughout the reaction}} kept the mixture even.
    The rainfall {{over the week}} filled the creek.
    The slow compaction {{over thousands of years}} turned the mud into rock.
    The wet weather {{until Friday}} delayed the practical.

Frequency (*How often?*)
------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **at**
     - :qualifier:`at times`
     - Intermittent / occasional occurrence
   * -
     - :qualifier:`at each high tide`
     - Cyclic natural repetition
   * -
     - :qualifier:`at the end of each trial`
     - Event-triggered repetition
   * -
     - :qualifier:`at steady intervals`
     - Regular, evenly spaced pattern
   * - **before**
     - :qualifier:`before every practical`
     - Interval preceding a repeating event
   * - **in**
     - :qualifier:`in each lesson`
     - Recurrence per structured unit
   * - **on**
     - :qualifier:`on every school day`
     - Routine schedule repetition
   * -
     - :qualifier:`on every Monday`
     - Weekly calendar recurrence
   * - **per**
     - :qualifier:`per day`
     - Standard rate or unit frequency


Time Qualifiers: Frequency
---------------------------------

.. textselect::

    The equipment checks {{on every school day}} kept the results accurate.
    The cloud cover {{at times}} blocked the sunlight.
    The waves {{at each high tide}} moved the sand.
