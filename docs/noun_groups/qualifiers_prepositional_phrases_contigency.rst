================================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Contingency)
================================================

| A **contingency qualifier** is a prepositional phrase that comes after a noun and adds information about the circumstances that something depends on or happens in spite of.
| Contingency qualifiers have two sub-functions, each with its own probe questions:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Condition**
     - *Under what condition?*
     - *in case of, in the absence of, in the event of*
   * - **Concession**
     - *Despite what?*
     - *despite, in spite of, regardless of*

----

Condition (*Under what condition?*)
------------------------------------

Condition qualifiers identify a circumstance or possible event under which something occurs, applies or is prepared for.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **in case of**
     - :qualifier:`in case of fire`
     - Possible event that preparation is made for
   * - **in the absence of**
     - :qualifier:`in the absence of light`
     - Condition in which something is missing
   * - **in the event of**
     - :qualifier:`in the event of a spill`
     - Possible event that may trigger a response


Contingency Qualifiers: Condition
----------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The fire blanket {{in case of fire}} hangs beside the exit.
    The sand bucket {{in the event of a spill}} sits under the sink.
    The spare batteries {{in case of a power cut}} are stored in the cupboard.
    The seedling growth {{in the absence of light}} was recorded in a table.
    The reaction {{in the absence of a catalyst}} took two hours.

----

Concession (*Despite what?*)
-----------------------------

Concession qualifiers identify a circumstance that might be expected to prevent or limit something but does not change the stated outcome.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **despite**
     - :qualifier:`despite the drought`
     - Obstacle that does not prevent an outcome
   * - **in spite of**
     - :qualifier:`in spite of the freezing wind`
     - Same as *despite*, slightly more emphatic
   * - **regardless of**
     - :qualifier:`regardless of its size`
     - Factor that the outcome does not depend on


Contingency Qualifiers: Concession
-----------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The cactus growth {{despite the drought}} amazed the botanists.
    The lichen survival {{in spite of the freezing wind}} surprised the explorers.
    The bird migration {{despite the storm}} began in April.
    The pendulum's swing time {{regardless of the bob's mass}} was measured with a stopwatch.
    The rock's density {{regardless of its size}} is measured in grams per cubic centimetre.

----
