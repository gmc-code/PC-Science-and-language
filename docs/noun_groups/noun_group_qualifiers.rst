========================================
Noun Group: Qualifiers
========================================

Adding Qualifiers to Noun Groups
-----------------------------------

| A **Qualifier** is a group of words that comes **after the noun** and gives extra information to define it more precisely.
| A Qualifier is often a prepositional phrase (e.g. *in the beaker*, *at high temperature*, *of the solution*), but can also take other forms such as embedded clauses (*that was heated*, *containing the mixture*.)

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 40 35

   * - Qualifier Type
     - Structural Example
     - Probe Question
   * - Prepositional Phrase
     - the water :qualifier:`in the beaker`
     - Where is it located?
   * - Finite Relative Clause
     - the acid :qualifier:`that turned pink`
     - Which specific one did what?
   * - Non-Finite *-ing* Clause
     - the gas :qualifier:`rising from the flask`
     - What action is it doing right now?
   * - Non-Finite *-ed* Clause
     - the liquid :qualifier:`heated by the burner`
     - What was done to it?
   * - Infinitive Clause
     - the step :qualifier:`to follow next`
     - What action is assigned to it?
   * - Adjectival Phrase
     - a sample :qualifier:`pure enough for testing`
     - In what state or condition is it?

   :instructions: Identify the Qualifier type (post-modifier) used in each noun group.
   :solution:

   The copper strip :qualifier:`that dissolved in the acid`: Finite Relative Clause
