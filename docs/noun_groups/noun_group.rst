========================================
Noun Groups
========================================

The Noun Group
--------------------------------

| A Noun Group is a group of words centered around a core noun (called the Thing).
| Every word added before or after that core noun has a specific job to do: it might describe its qualities, classify its type, or add more information about it.

----

| Probe questions can be asked to identify each part of the noun group.

.. list-table::
    :header-rows: 1
    :widths: 14 14 14 18 18 26 18

    * - **Element**
      - Pointer
      - Numerative
      - Describer
      - Classifier
      - :participantp:`Thing`
      - Qualifier
    * - **Probe Question**
      - Which?
      - How many?
      - What's it like?
      - What type or kind?
      - :participant:`Who or what?`
      - What more?
    * - **Word(s)**
      - The
      - 5
      - small
      - marble
      - :participant:`chips`
      - in the conical flask
    * - **Functional Role**
      - Identifying specific ones
      - Quantity
      - Size/ quality
      - Material/ sub-type
      - :participant:`The core noun`
      - Location/ context


.. wordordering::
    :instructions: Reorder the following parts of a noun group
    :delimiter: ,
    :theme: light

    Pointer, Numerative, Describer, Classifier, Thing,  Qualifier


.. classifying::
    :instructions: Select the correct probe question for each part of the noun group.
    :solution:
    :nosort:

    Pointer: Which?
    Numerative: How many?
    Describer: What's it like?
    Classifier: What type or kind?
    Thing: Who or what?
    Qualifier: What more?

The Pre-modifiers, the Thing, and Post-modifiers
------------------------------------------------------

| Consider the noun group: *The 5 small marble chips in the conical flask*
| It can be broken up into 3 sections:

.. list-table::
    :header-rows: 1
    :widths: 45 10 45
    :class: large-table

    * - :premodifierp:`Pre-modifiers (Before the noun)`
      - :participantp:`Noun`
      - :postmodifierp:`Post-modifiers (After the noun)`
    * - :premodifierp:`The 5 small marble`
      - :participantp:`chips`
      - :postmodifierp:`in the conical flask`

Example 1
--------------------------------------------

| Analyze the following noun group used in tectonic theory:
| **"The dense oceanic crust at the subduction zone"**

.. classifying::
    :instructions: Select the correct grammatical function for each part of the noun group.
    :solution:
    :nosort:

    The: Pointer
    dense: Describer
    oceanic: Classifier
    crust: Thing
    at the subduction zone: Qualifier


.. cloze::
    :instructions: Complete the following sentences by dragging the correct word into the blanks.
    :shuffle:

    Pointer: @@The@@ — Identifies the specific crust being observed in this tectonic context.
    Describer: @@dense@@ — Describes a physical property.
    Classifier: @@oceanic@@ — Categorizes the crustal material, distinguishing it from continental crust.
    Thing: @@crust@@ — The central participant that the other words are specifying.
    Qualifier: @@at the subduction zone@@ — Provides the precise location where the action is occurring.
