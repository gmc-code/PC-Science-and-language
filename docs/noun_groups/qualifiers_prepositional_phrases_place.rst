=========================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Place)
=========================================

| Place qualifiers are used to describe the location of something.
| Place qualifiers answer one of four core probe questions depending on their grammatical sub-function:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Position**
     - *Where?*
     - *above, against, among, at, behind, below, beneath, beside, between, by, in, in front of, inside, near, next to, on, opposite, outside, over, under, within*
   * - **Direction**
     - *Where to?*
     - *down, into, onto, to, towards, up*
   * - **Path**
     - *Which way?*
     - *across, along, around, over, past, through*
   * - **Origin**
     - *Where from?*
     - *away from, from, off, out of*

Position (*Where?*)
-------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **above**
     - :qualifier:`above the flame`
     - Higher than, without touching
   * - **against**
     - :qualifier:`against the wall`
     - In contact with a surface
   * - **among**
     - :qualifier:`among the pebbles`
     - Surrounded by several objects
   * - **at**
     - :qualifier:`at the bench`
     - Specific point or station
   * - **behind**
     - :qualifier:`behind the safety screen`
     - At the back of an object
   * - **below**
     - :qualifier:`below the surface`
     - Lower than a reference level
   * - **beneath**
     - :qualifier:`beneath the soil`
     - Directly underneath, often covered
   * - **beside**
     - :qualifier:`beside the balance`
     - At the side of an object
   * - **between**
     - :qualifier:`between the two layers`
     - In the space separating two objects
   * - **by**
     - :qualifier:`by the window`
     - Close to, at the side of
   * - **in**
     - :qualifier:`in the beaker`
     - Enclosed within a container or space
   * - **in front of**
     - :qualifier:`in front of the class`
     - Facing or ahead of an object
   * - **inside**
     - :qualifier:`inside the fume cupboard`
     - Contained within boundaries
   * - **near**
     - :qualifier:`near the heat source`
     - Short distance from an object
   * - **next to**
     - :qualifier:`next to the sink`
     - Directly adjacent
   * - **on**
     - :qualifier:`on the bench`
     - Resting on a surface
   * - **opposite**
     - :qualifier:`opposite the door`
     - Facing across a space
   * - **outside**
     - :qualifier:`outside the classroom`
     - Beyond the boundaries of a space
   * - **over**
     - :qualifier:`over the Bunsen burner`
     - Directly above
   * - **under**
     - :qualifier:`under the microscope`
     - Directly below
   * - **within**
     - :qualifier:`within the cell`
     - Inside the limits of a structure

--------------------------

    The thermometer {{in the beaker}} shows forty degrees.
    The goggles {{beside the balance}} belong to Sam.
    The safety screen {{behind the Bunsen burner}} protects the class.
    The plant {{by the window}} grew taller.
    The slide {{under the microscope}} shows onion cells.

Direction (*Where to?*)
-----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **down**
     - :qualifier:`down the drain`
     - Movement to a lower place
   * - **into**
     - :qualifier:`into the flask`
     - Movement to the inside of a space
   * - **onto**
     - :qualifier:`onto the slide`
     - Movement to the top of a surface
   * - **to**
     - :qualifier:`to the laboratory`
     - Movement towards a destination
   * - **towards**
     - :qualifier:`towards the light`
     - Movement in the direction of a target
   * - **up**
     - :qualifier:`up the stem`
     - Movement to a higher place

Path (*Which way?*)
-------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **across**
     - :qualifier:`across the leaf surface`
     - From one side to the other
   * - **along**
     - :qualifier:`along the riverbank`
     - Following the length of something
   * - **around**
     - :qualifier:`around the Sun`
     - Following a curved or circular route
   * - **over**
     - :qualifier:`over the wall`
     - Passing above an obstacle
   * - **past**
     - :qualifier:`past the sensor`
     - Passing beside without stopping
   * - **through**
     - :qualifier:`through the membrane`
     - Passing from one side to the other via the inside
