=========================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Angle)
=========================================

Angle qualifiers are prepositional phrases that come after a noun
and indicate the source of information or the perspective from which
something is considered.

Angle qualifiers can be divided into two sub-functions:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions and Phrases
   * - **Source**
     - *According to whom or what?*
     - *according to, in the words of, on the authority of*
   * - **Viewpoint**
     - *From whose perspective?*
     - *from the perspective of, from the standpoint of, in the eyes of, in the view of, to*

----

Source (*According to whom or what?*)
-------------------------------------

Source qualifiers identify the person, group, or source from which
information or a claim originates.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **according to**
     - :qualifier:`according to the weather station`
     - Identifies the source of information
   * - **in the words of**
     - :qualifier:`in the words of the geologist`
     - Identifies whose words are used
   * - **on the authority of**
     - :qualifier:`on the authority of the research team`
     - Identifies the authority supporting a claim


Angle Qualifiers: Source
------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The rainfall reading {{according to the weather station}} was printed in the newspaper.
    The account {{in the words of the geologist}} was displayed on the museum wall.
    The recommendation {{on the authority of the research team}} was included in the report.
    The definition {{according to the textbook}} is printed in bold.
    The description {{in the words of the explorer}} was copied into the journal.

----

Viewpoint (*From whose perspective?*)
-------------------------------------

Viewpoint qualifiers identify the person, group, or perspective
from which something is understood, described, or evaluated.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **from the perspective of**
     - :qualifier:`from the perspective of a chemist`
     - Identifies a person's professional or conceptual perspective
   * - **from the standpoint of**
     - :qualifier:`from the standpoint of energy transfer`
     - Identifies a particular conceptual approach
   * - **in the eyes of**
     - :qualifier:`in the eyes of the judges`
     - Identifies how a person or group judges something
   * - **in the view of**
     - :qualifier:`in the view of most scientists`
     - Identifies an opinion or judgement
   * - **to**
     - :qualifier:`to an observer on Earth`
     - Identifies how something appears to an observer


Angle Qualifiers: Viewpoint
---------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The explanation {{from the perspective of a chemist}} was written on the board.
    The summary {{from the standpoint of energy transfer}} filled one page.
    The best display {{in the eyes of the judges}} was made from recycled bottles.
    The claim {{in the view of most scientists}} was included in the report.
    The Moon {{to an observer on Earth}} was photographed every night for a month.

----
