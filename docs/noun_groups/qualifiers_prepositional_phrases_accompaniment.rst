=================================================
Qualifiers: Prepositional Phrases (Accompaniment)
=================================================

| Accompaniment qualifiers explain the relationship between two or more things.
| Accompaniment qualifiers answer one of five core probe questions depending on their grammatical sub-function:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 25 55

   * - Sub-Category
     - Probe Question
     - Typical Prepositions
   * - **Company**
     - *With whom?*
     - *alongside, along with, in the company of, with, without*
   * - **Combination**
     - *Combined with what?*
     - *in combination with, in conjunction with, together with, with*
   * - **Addition**
     - *In addition to what?*
     - *as well as, in addition to, including, plus*
   * - **Exclusion**
     - *Except what?*
     - *apart from, aside from, excluding, except, except for, other than*
   * - **Substitution**
     - *Instead of what?*
     - *in lieu of, in place of, instead of, rather than*

----

.. note::

   **Some prepositions end in -ing.**
   Words like *including* and *excluding* look like verbs, but in a
   prepositional phrase they work as prepositions. They link a noun to the
   noun that comes after them and do not describe an action.

   * The total :qualifier:`including the roots` (the total *with* the roots)
   * The average :qualifier:`excluding the first reading` (the average *without* the first reading)

   **Quick check:** if the word is followed by a noun phrase and the whole
   phrase qualifies a noun, it is a preposition. If you can swap it for
   *with* or *without* and the meaning stays close, that confirms it.

   You will meet more -ing prepositions in other sets, such as *owing to*,
   *barring* and *failing*.

Company (*With whom?*)
----------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **alongside**
     - :qualifier:`alongside the control group`
     - Side by side with another group
   * - **along with**
     - :qualifier:`along with the teacher`
     - Together with others in an activity
   * - **in the company of**
     - :qualifier:`in the company of the technician`
     - Formal statement of being accompanied
   * - **with**
     - :qualifier:`with a lab partner`
     - Person or group taking part too
   * - **without**
     - :qualifier:`without a supervisor`
     - Person or group who is absent


Accompaniment Qualifiers: Company
---------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The students {{with a lab partner}} finished the practical in twenty minutes.
    The seedlings {{alongside the control group}} were given the same type of soil.
    The class {{along with the teacher}} walked down to the creek.
    The group {{in the company of the technician}} learned how to light a Bunsen burner.
    The group {{without a supervisor}} was not allowed to heat the acid.

Combination (*Combined with what?*)
-----------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **in combination with**
     - :qualifier:`in combination with oxygen`
     - Two factors acting as a pair
   * - **in conjunction with**
     - :qualifier:`in conjunction with warmth and water`
     - Formal link between two factors
   * - **together with**
     - :qualifier:`together with water and oxygen`
     - Factors joined to produce one result
   * - **with**
     - :qualifier:`with distilled water`
     - Substance mixed or joined with another


Accompaniment Qualifiers: Combination
-------------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    Iron {{together with water and oxygen}} forms rust.
    Magnesium {{in combination with oxygen}} forms a white powder.
    Seeds {{in conjunction with warmth and water}} begin to germinate.
    Salt {{with distilled water}} forms a clear solution.
    Sunlight {{together with carbon dioxide and water}} allows the leaf to make glucose.

Addition (*In addition to what?*)
---------------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **as well as**
     - :qualifier:`as well as water`
     - Extra item alongside the main one
   * - **in addition to**
     - :qualifier:`in addition to glucose`
     - Formal statement of an extra item
   * - **including**
     - :qualifier:`including the roots`
     - Extra item counted as part of the whole
   * - **plus**
     - :qualifier:`plus the water`
     - Extra item added on


Accompaniment Qualifiers: Addition
----------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    Oxygen {{in addition to glucose}} is a product of photosynthesis.
    The minerals {{as well as water}} travel up the stem.
    The whole plant {{including the roots}} was weighed on the balance.
    The beaker {{plus the water}} had a mass of two hundred and fifty grams.
    The food chain {{including the top predator}} has four feeding levels.

----

Exclusion (*Except what?*)
--------------------------

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **apart from**
     - :qualifier:`apart from the one in the cupboard`
     - Item set aside from the rest
   * - **aside from**
     - :qualifier:`aside from Mercury and Venus`
     - Item set to one side
   * - **excluding**
     - :qualifier:`excluding the first trial`
     - Item deliberately left out of a count
   * - **except**
     - :qualifier:`except mercury`
     - Single item that does not fit
   * - **except for**
     - :qualifier:`except for the control`
     - Item that is the exception to a rule
   * - **other than**
     - :qualifier:`other than vinegar`
     - Anything not including the named item


Accompaniment Qualifiers: Exclusion
-----------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    All the plants {{apart from the one in the cupboard}} grew towards the window.
    All the planets {{aside from Mercury and Venus}} have at least one moon.
    All the metals {{except mercury}} are solid at room temperature.
    All the liquids {{other than vinegar}} were neutral when tested with indicator.
    The average speed {{excluding the first trial}} was twelve centimetres per second.
    All the test tubes {{except for the control}} turned cloudy when carbon dioxide bubbled through.

----

Substitution (*Instead of what?*)
---------------------------------

These qualifiers identify something that is replaced or something used in its
place.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 30 50

   * - Preposition
     - Example
     - Meaning / Context
   * - **in lieu of**
     - :qualifier:`in lieu of vinegar`
     - Formal expression meaning instead of
   * - **in place of**
     - :qualifier:`in place of the wooden ball`
     - Something that takes another's role
   * - **instead of**
     - :qualifier:`instead of litmus paper`
     - Something chosen rather than another
   * - **rather than**
     - :qualifier:`rather than distilled water`
     - Something chosen in preference to another


Accompaniment Qualifiers: Substitution
--------------------------------------

.. textselect::
    :instructions: Select the Qualifier of the noun group.
    :color: qualifier

    The red cabbage juice {{instead of litmus paper}} turned pink in the acid.
    The steel ball {{in place of the wooden ball}} rolled down the ramp.
    The lemon juice {{in lieu of vinegar}} still fizzed with the baking soda.
    The tap water {{rather than distilled water}} left white deposits in the kettle.
    The copper wire {{in place of the plastic rod}} completed the electrical circuit.

Accompaniment Qualifiers: Choose the Qualifier
----------------------------------------------

.. cloze::
    :instructions: Complete the following sentences by dragging the correct qualifiers into the blanks. Use the probe question in brackets to help you decide.

    The visitors @@in the company of a ranger@@ counted the frogs at the pond. (With whom? Which person or group is present too?)
    Baking soda @@together with vinegar@@ produces carbon dioxide. (Combined with what? Which two substances act together?)
    The digestive system @@including the small intestine@@ absorbs nutrients. (In addition to what? Which part is counted within the whole?)
    All the readings @@apart from the outlier@@ were used to calculate the average. (Except what? Which item is set aside from the rest?)
    The salt solution @@instead of tap water@@ made the egg float. (Instead of what? Which item was replaced?)
