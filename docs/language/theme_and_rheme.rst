=======================
Theme and Rheme
=======================

Theme and Rheme (often paired with Given and New) structure the flow of
information within clauses. Theme provides the starting point or orientation,
while Rheme develops the message by introducing core information and details.

1. Establishing the Groundwork with Theme
------------------------------------------

The Theme appears at the beginning of the clause and serves as the anchor for
the message. It informs the reader what the sentence is about or sets the
context for the information that follows.

.. admonition:: Examples
   :class: pro

   | **Photosynthesis** occurs in the chloroplasts of plant cells.
   | **The heart** pumps oxygenated blood throughout the body.
   | **Gravity** pulls objects toward the center of the Earth.

----

2. Delivering New Information in the Rheme
-------------------------------------------

The Rheme comprises the remainder of the clause. It carries the primary
informative weight, introducing new concepts, details, or elaborations that
build upon the foundation set by the Theme.

.. admonition:: Examples
   :class: pro

   | Photosynthesis **occurs in the chloroplasts of plant cells.**
   | The heart **pumps oxygenated blood throughout the body.**
   | Gravity **pulls objects toward the center of the Earth.**

----

3. Guiding Textual Flow and Progression
---------------------------------------

Effective explanations connect the Rheme of one sentence to the Theme of the
next. This creates a cohesive information chain, making complex ideas easier
for the reader to digest step by step.

.. admonition:: Examples
   :class: pro

   | Light energy hits **the chloroplasts**. **These organelles** absorb specific wavelengths.
   | The reaction produces **glucose**. **This sugar** provides energy for the cell.
   | Heat increases **particle movement**. **This kinetic energy** causes the gas to expand.
