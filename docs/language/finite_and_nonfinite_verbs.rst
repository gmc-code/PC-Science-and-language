========================================
Finite and Non-Finite Verbs
========================================

* **Finite verbs** are verbs that have a full time-stamped story.

* **Non-finite verbs** are verbs that don't have a full time-stamped story.

----

Finite Verbs (Time-stamped / Complete)
------------------------------------------

**Features:**
   - Has tense (past/present/future),
   - person agreement (singular/plural),
   - or modal obligation/possibility.

**Science Examples:** *reacts*, *boiled*, *is*, *will expand*, *must measure*

----

Non-Finite Verbs (Unanchored / Incomplete)
------------------------------------------

**Features:** Includes
    - infinitives (*to + verb*),
    - present participles (*-ing*),
    - and past participles (*-ed/en*).

They cannot serve as the main action verb on its own.

**Science Examples:** *to react*, *boiling*, *measured* (e.g., *"the measured liquid"*)

----

.. classifying::
    :instructions: Classify each verb as a Finite Verb or Non-Finite Verb.
    :solution:
    :delimiter: |

    The magnesium :process:`is reacting` with hydrochloric acid. | Finite Verb
    The chemicals need time :process:`to react` in the tube. | Non-Finite Verb
    The mixture :process:`boiled` rapidly over the flame. | Finite Verb
    Place the test tube into the :process:`boiling` water. | Non-Finite Verb
    The solution :process:`is` neutral on the pH scale. | Finite Verb
    Pour the :process:`measured` liquid into the flask. | Non-Finite Verb
    The gas :process:`will expand` as temperature rises. | Finite Verb
    You :process:`must measure` the mass before heating. | Finite Verb
